Moon Faire open world: pictures to make

/*N*/ prompts and /*NREF*/ reference pictures for the open-world map. Walking and exploring happen in the 3D open world. Fights stay on painted backdrops and are not part of this list.

Texture packs, not building reference photos

The open world paints flat pictures straight onto simple 3D shapes. It does not model a building by looking at photos of it. So the useful pictures are of two kinds:

  • Texture packs (sets A, B and E): seamless squares of ground, cloth, wood, stone and plants. One square of striped canvas covers every striped tent in every zone, at any size and from any side. This is what lets the world grow: a new zone reuses the packs and needs only a few new squares of its own.
  • Skin sheets (sets C and D): flat front views of the few things a pack cannot describe, such as a tent's door, a stall's counter with its goods, or the wagon. They work the way the fair folk's skins do.

How to make them

  1. For each prompt, attach the reference pictures shown on its card. They are all at the bottom of this page, each with a Save link, and in the zip that came with this page.
  2. Paste the prompt. Each one is complete, with its rules included.
  3. Save the result under the file name on the card.
  4. Zip a round together and send it back.

If a picture comes back with a lamp glow, a shadow, or perspective in it, ask for it again. Those three are what spoil a texture.
