How to walk

  • W A S D or the arrow keys move her the way the camera faces. She runs; hold Shift to walk, or turn Run off.
  • Drag the picture to turn the camera round her, or use Q and E. The wheel brings it closer or farther. It swings back behind her as she walks.
  • Tap or click the ground and she walks there.
  • Near someone, press Do it (or E, Space) to talk.
  • Map (or M) shows where she is. Tap a pin or anywhere on the land to go there.

What this is

  • The land is made from rules and the painted map, as the racing game's world is, in squares of 32 m round the camera, finer near her and coarser far off, made in the background as she goes.
  • The fair square and the six other painted places are built in 3D from the shapes their paintings were made from, with their paintings laid back onto those shapes.
  • The ground between them is patches of your own paintings, coloured by the painted map.
