What this test is

  • The fair square built in 3D, inside the open world, with four witches at their Light weight and all eleven fair folk at their lightest standing round it.
  • The four witches are four Jesters for now. The Fox's and the Ranger's Light versions are not saved as files yet, and Io's is being built. A real Fox at Light is about a quarter heavier than a Jester.
  • The line under the picture is the result: pictures a second on this device. 30 or more is comfortable.
  • Witches switches all four between Light, Lightest and Trim. Sharpness changes how many screen pixels are drawn.

How to move

  • Tap or click the ground and she walks there. Drag the picture to turn the camera.
  • On a keyboard: W A S D, Q and E to turn, the wheel to move the camera in and out.
  • Near someone, press Do it to talk. Map goes anywhere in the world.
