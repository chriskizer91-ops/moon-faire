Herbs and rest spots

Fights now start with the HP and MP the party has. She picks wild plants on the moor, Io makes them into herbs, and a rest spot refills everything for free. I played 67,200 stretches of fights from a rest spot, with all eight testers, to see how many plants the moor needs and how far apart the rest spots can be.

What I need from you

  1. Sparing, steady or generous with the plants. The three are just below.
  2. An MP herb gives 20 MP. Yes, or smaller? At 10 she eats twice as many.
  3. A witch who fainted gets up after a won fight with 10% of her HP. I made that up. Yes, or something else?

How herbs work, as I built them

  • An HP herb gives 60 HP. That is half the Jester's own heal of 120, the weakest heal in the game, by your rule. Io's Mend gives 250.
  • After a fight she eats as many as she likes, on whoever needs them.
  • In a fight eating a herb takes that witch's turn. The testers only do it when a friend is in trouble and Io cannot mend.
  • Spells are for fights. Io cannot Mend between fights. If she could, MP herbs would be worth far more than HP herbs.
  • A rest spot refills all HP and MP. It gives no herbs.
  • Losing still costs nothing: she wakes at the last rest spot.

What I found

  • MP runs out, not HP. Mend turns 10 MP into 250 HP, so a party with MP is a party with HP. With no herbs at all, a careful player wins 31 fights in a row in the first ring, 9 in the third and 4 in the last. This is why your two MP finds matter now.
  • The far rings are hard on a careless player. In the last ring she wins about 3 fights from a rest spot whatever she is carrying. Before, with fresh fights, she lost two or three walking fights in the whole game. If anyone is to see the whole game, the far rings want rest spots close together, or gentler foes on the main path.
  • The second play style still is not a real choice. I made "bold" a player who only tops up to 60% HP and 40% MP. She saves almost no herbs and is much less safe: in the last ring with lots of plants she wins 8 fights on an unlucky run against 40+ for the careful one. Topping up fully is simply right. I would drop the two styles and keep five testers, unless you want "a player who under-heals" as a tester in her own right.
  • The earlier numbers were the best case. The levelling hours, the finds and the big-bad table all assumed fresh fights. They need running again once you have picked, and the big bad should be met straight from a rest spot.
