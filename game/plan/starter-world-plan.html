The map

The game in six lines

A thin story to hang the game on. It is yours to replace.

  • The Ranger, the Fox and Io are at the fair at dusk. Wisps drift in among the tents.
  • The wisps make off with the twelve lamps of the captain's airship. He cannot fly it dark.
  • The three go out onto the moor to win the lamps back, one fight at a time.
  • At the standing stones they find the Jester holding off a Shadow Wraith alone. She joins.
  • With all twelve lamps lit, the captain flies them to where the wisps were carrying the light: a dead airship beacon on a crag.
  • At the top, a great wraith sits on a hoard of stolen light. The Jester ends it, and the curtain falls.

Zone by zone

1. The Moon Faire

party level 1 to 2foes level 1 to 2

The tent town at dusk. Safe to walk: no walking fights here, only three set fights and the ring.

  • W1The fair square. The game starts here. Lantern posts, bunting, tents all round. First fight, the teaching one: two wisps drift in. Rest and save at the well.
  • W2The stall row and the sparring ring. A lane of stalls ending at a roped ring and the fair gate. Second fight: three wisps among the stalls. Three or four stalls can be talked to. The ring gives a practice bout whenever you like (two wisps at your level), so nobody can ever get stuck short of a level.
  • W3The mooring field. The captain under his moored airship. Third fight: three wisps at level 2, while the rest carry off the ship's lamps. The party reaches level 2 here. Talk to the captain: he asks for the lamps. Each lamp won back lights up on the ship, so the painting itself shows how close you are.

2. The wilds

party level 2 to 4foes level 2 to 6

A moonlit moor along an old road, the fair glowing behind. Walking fights come evenly, about one every 14 metres walked. Each scene holds a fixed number of lamps: its first few walking fights each carry one, and one sits with a set fight at the landmark.

  • W4The Last Lantern. The fair's last lamp post, then open moor. 3 lamps. Walking: three or four wisps, level 2 to 3. Set fight at the post: four wisps, level 3.
  • W5The standing stones. A ring of old stones across the road. 4 lamps. Walking: wisps, or a Shadow Wraith with two wisps, level 3 to 4. Set fight: the Jester is already fighting a Shadow Wraith and two wisps (level 4). She is in your party from the first turn of it. This is where the player learns the taunt and Sealed Fate.
  • W6The still pool and the broken arch. The end of the road. 5 lamps. A camp to rest and save. Walking: a Shadow Wraith with two or four wisps, or five wisps, level 4 to 5. Set fight under the arch: two Shadow Wraiths and two wisps, level 5.
  • W7The hollow. Hidden, down a gap behind the stones, open once the Jester has joined. Five wisps at level 6 guard a healing amulet (all healing 5% stronger). Foes this far above you are always the weak kind, as you ruled.

The shortest way to all twelve lamps is out to the pool and back, with a little wandering at the pool and the stones: about 14 wild fights. The party is level 4 when the last lamp goes on the ship.

3. The Dark Beacon

party level 4 to 5foes level 5 to 6

A dead beacon for airships, on a crag that can only be reached by air. Deep night.

  • W8The landing ledge. The airship moored against the rock, all twelve lamps lit. The captain waits here. No fights. Rest and save. He will fly you back to the fair if you ask.
  • W9The beacon stair. A long climb round the crag. Walking: Shadow Wraiths with wisps, level 5 to 6. Off the path, a charm (every witch's HP 2% higher). Level 5 arrives after about one and a half climbs.
  • F5The lamp room. The door at the top opens at level 5, the boss's level. The great wraith, two Shadow Wraiths and two wisps, level 5. No walking here: the fight begins at the door.

  • What a foe gives: a wisp 10, a Shadow Wraith 40, the great wraith 400, each growing 20% for every level of the foe. The whole party shares it and levels together.
  • Total to reach each level: 80, then 400, then 1,150, then 2,000.

  • Losing a fight costs nothing: you wake at the last place you rested.

The gate

The twelve lamps. The captain will not fly until every lamp is back on his ship. A lamp only comes from winning a fight in the wilds, and each of the three wild scenes holds its own share (3, 4 and 5), so the party has to go all the way out, at rising levels, and come back.

  • It uses the airship and the captain, and it needs no level number on a door.
  • The lamps light up one by one on the moored ship in the mooring field, and a small row of lamps on screen keeps count.
  • Measured: the party is level 4 when the twelfth lamp comes home, in all 2,000 playthroughs.

Who is in the party, and when

  • Fights 1 to 7The Ranger, the Fox and Io. Your engine says these three win every small fight on their own.
  • Fight 8 or 9The Jester joins at the standing stones, at the party's level. From here it is all four.
  • The captainNot in the party. He stands at the mooring field, then at the landing ledge. He talks, he keeps the lamp count, and he flies the ship.
  • WalkingOne witch leads and the others follow her path. Any of them can lead.
  • Outside fightsNo spells. They walk, run, talk, wave, bow, laugh and do their other body moves.

How long it plays

  • Fights: 25 to 45 minutes. About 24 fights holding about 240 witch turns and 85 foe turns. That is an estimate at 4 to 9 seconds a witch turn. It cannot be measured until the real spell animations play in a fight.
  • Walking, talking and the flight: 12 to 15 minutes.
  • First play: about 45 to 55 minutes, inside your 40 to 60.

How the witches keep their look

  • Their light rig and their models are used exactly as they are. The paintings are made to suit them.
  • Every painting is lit the same way the witches are: warm light from the viewer's side and the right, the moon behind and to the upper left, cool sky low on the left, warm earth underfoot.
  • Auros hangs in the same place in every sky, because it barely moves. That is also where the witches' lilac edge light comes from, so they will always match it.
  • The first check, before anything else is painted: the Jester standing in the pilot painting beside herself in her own theatre, at the same size.
  • Speed is for your laptop to judge. No Claude session can measure it: the test browser runs at under two frames a second.

Your calls

Ten choices. A is my pick each time and is what the plan above uses. Reply with only the ones you want changed, for example "3B, 8C". Anything you do not mention stays A.

  1. Dusk or night?

    A One evening that darkens as they go: dusk at the fair, night on the moor, deep night at the beacon.

    B Dusk everywhere. C Night everywhere.

  2. Does Auros show in the paintings?

    A Yes, in every sky, always in the same spot, upper left behind the scene.

    B Only in the wilds and at the beacon. C Never shown, only its light.

  3. When does each witch join?

    A The Ranger, the Fox and Io from the start. The Jester at the standing stones.

    B All four from the start. C One at a time, starting with one witch alone (this needs new measuring).

  4. The gate to zone 3

    A The airship's twelve lamps, won back in the wilds.

    B A plain rule: the captain flies at level 4. C The Jester joining opens it.

  5. Fights in town

    A Both: three set fights as wisps come into the fair, and the ring for practice bouts.

    B Only the wisps. C Only the ring.

  6. The foes

    A Envoi's wraith family only: the wisp, the Shadow Wraith, the great wraith. A wisp carrying a ship's lamp is the same wisp with a lamp.

    B Add one new simple foe for the moor, such as a ragflag (a torn fair banner come alive). C Add Envoi's Gloamwing as well.

  7. Names

    A Placeholders for now, all kept in one data file so you can rename anything in a minute.

    B You name the game, the zones and the places before the paintings are asked for.

  8. The last fight

    A The great wraith, two Shadow Wraiths and two wisps. Wins at level 5: 6 / 81 / 100%. A level short: 0 / 3 / 92%.

    B The great wraith and two Shadow Wraiths: 11 / 100 / 100%, and 1 / 66 / 97% a level short. C The great wraith, one Shadow Wraith and two wisps: 17 / 100 / 100%, and 1 / 86 / 100% a level short.

  9. Between fights

    A Every fight starts with full HP and MP, as in the blob battle, so the balance you approved holds exactly.

    B Hurts carry over and the rest spots heal. This changes the balance and would need measuring again.

  10. The fair folk

    A Voices from inside the stalls and tents, with no new models. The only people you see are the five that exist.

    B Leave talking to the captain alone until more models exist.
