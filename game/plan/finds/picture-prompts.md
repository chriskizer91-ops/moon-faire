# Moon Faire: pictures for the thirteen new item cards

Made on November 14, 2026 by the "Levelling" session for task board S5. The five cards the game already has were painted on `art/items/items.png`. These follow that sheet's prompt (`game/world/pictures/prompts.md`, I1) word for word where they can, so the new cards sit beside the old ones. For each picture: attach the files named, in that order, and paste the prompt. Save the result under the name given.

**The things are stand-ins, like the cards' names.** If a card changes, its line here changes with it. No witches, and no people, in any of them.

## I2  Item sheet 2

Cards for: The stubborn candle; The straight spark; The idle needle; The brass snuffer; The winter blackthorn; The warm oil flask.

Attach: items.png, then style-fair-square.jpg.  Shape: 3:2, 1536 x 1024 or larger.  Save as: `items2.png`.

```
One sheet of six small item pictures for my fantasy role-playing game, Moon Faire. The game cuts the sheet into six equal squares and shows each one on an item card.

Output exactly ONE landscape image, 3:2, 1536 x 1024 pixels or larger, laid out as two rows of three equal squares. Draw no lines, borders or gaps between the squares: the same cloth runs under all six. Each thing lies alone in the exact middle of its own square and fills about 60% of it. Nothing crosses from one square into the next.

I am attaching two images.
- Image 1 (items.png) is the first item sheet from this same game. Match it exactly: the same near-black plum wool cloth, the same soft cool light from the upper left, the same size of thing in its square, the same finish.
- Image 2 (style-fair-square.jpg) is a finished painting from this same game. Match its craft, its finish and its realism. Take nothing else from it.

STYLE. A realistic, richly detailed, hand-painted picture with believable scale and painterly but detailed surfaces: real wax, tin, brass, steel, stone, wool, paper and leather. The same hand as the attached paintings. Not pixel art, not a cartoon, not anime, no drawn outlines.

THE CLOTH AND THE LIGHT. One 3:2 sheet of plain near-black plum wool cloth, with no folds, seams, borders, dividing lines or lettering, read as two rows of three equal squares; each thing lies alone in the exact middle of its own square and fills about 60% of it, all under the same soft cool light from the upper left with a short soft shadow to the lower right, and any glow stays inside its own square.

THE SIX THINGS, left to right, the top row first and then the bottom row:
1. Top left, the stubborn candle: a short, fat tallow candle stub in a dented tin holder, burning with a small steady gold flame that leans a little to the left.
2. Top middle, the straight spark: one knapped grey flint arrowhead lying flat, its edges finely chipped, with a single bright white-gold spark and a short straight trail of light leaving its point.
3. Top right, the idle needle: one long bright steel sewing needle lying at a slant, unthreaded, with a cold blue glint along its length and at its eye.
4. Bottom left, the brass snuffer: one old brass candle snuffer, a small cone-shaped cap on a long thin twisted handle, lying at a slant, the brass worn bright at the rim, with a thin curl of pale smoke rising from under the cap.
5. Bottom middle, the winter blackthorn: one short dark sprig of blackthorn with long sharp thorns and a cluster of small white five-petalled flowers, faintly luminous.
6. Bottom right, the warm oil flask: one small dented tin oil flask with a cork stopper and a wire handle, standing upright, a soft amber warmth showing through a worn seam and on the cloth beside it.

LEAVE OUT. Hands, people, creatures, lettering that can be read, numbers, labels, price tags, frames and shadows of anything outside the picture.
```

The game cuts it into: `stubborn-candle.png`, `straight-spark.png`, `idle-needle.png`, `brass-snuffer.png`, `winter-blackthorn.png`, `warm-oil-flask.png`.
