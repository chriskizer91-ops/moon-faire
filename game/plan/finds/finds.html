Finds for the bigger game

Level 20 is the top, so what she finds is now the only thing that makes the last gate easier. Here are 18 finds, three in each of the six rings: one on the way, one off the path, one hidden. Five are the cards the game already has. Thirteen are new, and every new name and card is a stand-in for you to change.

What I need from you

  1. Full strength or half strength. The table just below shows both.
  2. The two MP finds. Cold lantern glass and The cut thread do nothing the runs can see, because every fight starts with full MP. Keep them as they are, or shall I give them a new job and measure it?
  3. The names and cards. Say what to change, or have them rewritten. The pictures to paint are at the bottom.

What the measuring found

  • HP is the strongest thing a find can give. 2% more HP is worth +3 points at the last gate, and 5% is worth +7. 5% stronger healing is worth +3. This is what your combat notes said, so the HP finds stay small.
  • MP finds do nothing in one fight. 5% more MP: 0 points. The Fox's spells 5% cheaper: 0. Nobody runs out of MP in a single fight.
  • The curtain weight is the big one. Sealed Fate taking 25% from the other foes is worth +10 points alone. It is the hidden find of ring 5, and it is meant to feel like a prize.
  • Two finds are worth more than the runs show. The pretend player who pays attention never calls the Fox's ghost and seldom leans on Bloom, so The spare outline and The fourth seed only show up for the any-button player. A real player who uses them will get more.
  • Left out on purpose. The Ranger's gem ready a turn sooner was worth +13 points alone, which is too much. The Jester catching a fourth blow helped nobody. Getting up for the bow at 50% changed no result.
  • If the story ever adds a fight harder than the last gate, the full set is what makes it winnable. The hardest fight in your combat notes goes from 43% to 98% for a player paying attention with all eighteen.
