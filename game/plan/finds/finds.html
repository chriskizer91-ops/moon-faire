Moon Faire, task board S5

Finds for the bigger game

Eighteen finds, three in each of the six rings: one on the way, one off the path, one hidden. Five are the cards the game already had and thirteen are new. All eighteen are painted now.

What you decided on November 15

  • Half strength. Every percentage on this page is half of what I first proposed.
  • The names and cards work. They are no longer stand-ins.
  • The two MP finds stay, because fights will start with the MP she has, and she will have to refill it.
  • A big bad at level 21 or 22, against a party that stops at 20, so anyone can see the whole game but winning it takes real effort. Only an expert who has found everything should just barely win.

Against a big bad at level 21 or 22

This is today's last fight (the great wraith, two Shadow Wraiths and two wisps) with its foes moved up, since the big bad itself is not designed yet. 3,000 fights for every number.

At level 21 a player paying attention wins 3% of the time with nothing and 13% with every find. An expert hardly notices: 91% and 97%.

At level 22 nobody but an expert wins at all, which is what you asked for. But an expert already wins 27% with nothing and 80% with every find, and that is well past "just barely". At level 23 even an expert with everything wins 1%.

So your aim sits between 22 and 23 for this line-up. A big bad at level 22 that is a little tougher than today's last fight would put the expert with everything at "just barely". That is a number to set when the big bad is designed, and when fights carry HP and MP over, which will change all of this.

The eighteen finds

"Alone at the last gate" is what one find is worth by itself there, in points of winning. Which piece of the map each ring is, and exactly where each find lies, are not decided here.

Ring 1 party level 2 to 5

Cold lantern glass

All four have 2.5% more MP.

A lantern's glass, found in the heather a long way from any post. The flame behind it answered the call one night and pulled the glass halfway after it. It is still cold to hold.

on the way Alone at the last gate: too small for the runs to see.

The stubborn candle new

All four have 1% more HP.

A candle stub from a fair stall, dropped in the heather half a mile out. The call took it this far and no further. It is still lit, and it leans the wrong way.

off the path Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

The straight spark new

The Ranger's arrows and gem shots hit 2.5% harder.

A flint arrowhead, wedged in a crack of the drystone wall. Struck on steel it throws one spark, and the spark flies dead straight. Nothing else out here does.

hidden for the Ranger. Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

Ring 2 party level 5 to 8

The cut thread

The Fox's spells cost 2.5% less MP.

One thread of the call, snipped by the Fox where it caught on a standing stone. Every flame on the moor has one, pulled tight toward the crag. Even cut, it still twitches that way.

on the way for the Fox. Alone at the last gate: too small for the runs to see.

The idle needle new

The Fox's Stitch and Pins and Needles hit 2.5% harder.

A long steel needle, stuck in the wood of a dead lamp post with nothing through its eye. The Fox says it is the one thing on this moor that is not being pulled anywhere.

off the path for the Fox. Alone at the last gate: too small for the runs to see.

The brass snuffer new

Every blow the Jester takes lays 2.5% more Fate on the foe.

A candle snuffer: a small brass cap on a long handle, made for one job. It gives a flame its ending, gently, and all at once. The Jester would not let anyone else carry it.

hidden for the Jester. Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

Ring 3 party level 8 to 11

The warm stone

All four have 2.5% more HP.

A stone from the edge of the farm lad's fire ring. That fire had someone feeding it all night, and it never once tried to leave. The stone keeps its warmth for hours.

on the way for Io. Alone at the last gate: +4 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

The winter blackthorn new

Io's Thorn and her Deity hit 2.5% harder.

A sprig of blackthorn in full white flower, long out of season. It grew where a wisp lay down and was warm for a while. Io keeps it in her hat band.

off the path for Io. Alone at the last gate: +1 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

The hollow amulet

All healing is 2.5% stronger.

It lay on the flat stone in the hollow, where nothing on the moor pulls. Nobody knows who left it there. It is warm the way a hand is warm, not the way a fire is.

hidden for Io. Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

Ring 4 party level 11 to 14

The warm oil flask new

All four have 1% more HP.

A tin flask of lamp oil, left at the foot of a post. Its lamp went east long ago. The oil stayed, and it has never gone cold.

on the way Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

The fourth seed new

Io's Bloom opens 4 times, up from 3.

A twist of brown paper from a seed-seller at some other fair, in some other year. The writing says three. There are four.

off the path for Io. Alone at the last gate: too small for the runs to see.

The paired amulet new

All healing is 2.5% stronger.

The same grey-green stone as the hollow amulet, and the same spiral, cut the other way round. Laid side by side the two carvings run into each other like one line.

hidden for Io. Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

Ring 5 party level 14 to 17

The Fox's ghost eats 3 blows, up from 2.

The shape of a person, cut out of an old fair handbill and pinned to a post. Nobody is printed on it. The Fox folded it very carefully and said it would do.

on the way for the Fox. Alone at the last gate: 0 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

The keeper's glove new

All four have 1% more HP.

One thick wool glove, scorched across the palm, the kind a lamp keeper wears to lift hot glass. There is only the one. It is a comfort to hold.

off the path Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

The curtain weight new

Sealed Fate takes 22.5% of their Life from the other foes, up from 20%.

A lead weight from the hem of a stage curtain. It is what makes a curtain fall straight, and fall everywhere at once. The Jester laughed for a long time when she found it.

hidden for the Jester. Alone at the last gate: +5 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

Ring 6 party level 17 to 20

The lit taper new

Io's Revive brings a friend back at 35% HP, up from 30%.

A long wax taper, the kind used to carry a flame from one lamp to the next. It was left burning in a crack of the rock, out of the wind. One flame can always light another.

on the way for Io. Alone at the last gate: +5 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

The keeper's charm

The Jester takes 2.5% less damage.

A small iron charm, hung from an empty lamp bracket on the side of the tower. Keepers once hung one on every bracket they trimmed, so the flame would know someone was coming back. This is the last one, and nobody came back.

off the path for the Jester. Alone at the last gate: +3 points paying attention, +1 pressing any button.

The far amulet new

All healing is 2.5% stronger.

The third spiral. Whoever carved the three left them a long walk apart, like markers on a road nobody remembers. This one was the furthest, and the hardest to reach.

hidden for Io. Alone at the last gate: +2 points paying attention, 0 pressing any button.

What the measuring found

  • HP is the strongest thing a find can give. At the sizes first proposed, 2% more HP was worth +3 points at the last gate and 5% was worth +7; 5% stronger healing was worth +3. This is what your combat notes said.
  • MP finds do nothing while every fight starts with full MP. Once fights start with the MP she has, they will matter. That is not measured yet.
  • The curtain weight is the big one. At 25% it was worth +10 points alone. It is the hidden find of ring 5, and it is meant to feel like a prize.
  • Two finds are worth more than the runs show. The pretend player who pays attention never calls the Fox's ghost and seldom leans on Bloom, so The spare outline and The fourth seed barely register. A real player who uses them will get more. The new testers you asked for should fix this.
  • Left out on purpose. The Ranger's gem ready a turn sooner was worth +13 points alone, which is too much. The Jester catching a fourth blow helped nobody. Getting up for the bow at 50% changed no result.
