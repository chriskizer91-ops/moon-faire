Who they are

Very careless

Presses any button that is lit, at any foe or friend. No plan at all. Never runs away.

Carelessthe same as before

Mostly hits things. Seals a foe when the game offers it, revives a fallen friend, sometimes heals. The same careless player as before.

Attentive, carefulthe same as before

Reads the next three turns. Turns a Heavy or Crush back with the Fox, mends anyone under half, keeps MP back for healing. The same attentive player as before.

Attentive, bold

The same reading, but she heals late (under about a third), spends her MP on hitting, and calls Io's Deity against two foes.

Focused, careful

Reads the next three turns, and uses every tool: calls the Fox's ghost when the Jester is low, quilts small foes in a crowd, and lays Bloom down when an Eclipse is coming.

Focused, bold

The same tools, but she heals late and spends freely on hitting.

Expert, carefulthe same as before

Reads the whole turn bar, uses every tool, and knows the fine points: lets the Jester take a Crush on purpose for the Fate, holds a seal for the right moment, tops the Jester up before a blow she could not survive. The same expert as before.

Expert, bold

The same fine points with thinner margins: heals later, keeps almost no MP back, and goes for the kill.

What I found

  • The two play styles only differ in the hard fights, and bold is simply the weaker one. At level 20 the bold attentive player wins the last fight 54% of the time against 80% for the careful one. In ordinary fights the two take the same number of turns and end with the same HP and MP. So today the styles are not a real second choice. I think they become one with the herbs: a thrifty player and a spender. I suggest we keep all eight for now and I rework the styles around herbs.
  • An ordinary fight is not free. After two Shadow Wraiths and two wisps two levels over, an attentive party has about 87% of its HP and 66% of its MP left. That is what the herbs will have to give back.
  • The focused player now uses the Fox's ghost, which the old attentive player never did. That is what lifts her from 3% to 35% at level 21.
  • Nobody runs away. None of the eight uses Slip Stitch, so a fight is won or lost. With herbs to protect, running may become worth testing.
