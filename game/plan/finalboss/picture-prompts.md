# Moon Faire: pictures for the final boss

Made on November 15, 2026 by the "Levelling" session with three Opus 5.5 helpers, for task board F11. Chris offered to
have reference pictures made from a rough visual idea. These are model sheets: the thing alone, seen from three sides,
for building it in code. **Every name is a stand-in and the idea is a proposal.** No witches and no people in any of them.

## B1  The fire that would not end (the one proposed)

Shape: 3:2, 1536 x 1024 or larger.  Save as: `final-fire-sheet.png`.

```
Model sheet of a single towering fantasy boss for my role-playing game, Moon Faire, full figure, shown three times side by side on one sheet: front view, three-quarter view, back view. Each view is the same size and stands on the same ground line.

WHAT IT IS. An ancient beacon fire that would not go out, grown into a black sun. A floating black sphere like an eclipse, about a quarter of the figure's height, with a thin glowing rose-white rim. Around the sphere, a ring of thirty or so long flat flame-shaped blades that glow deep crimson at the root and pale rose-white at the tips, like a corona. Three engraved old-gold iron rings orbit the sphere at different tilts, like the rings of an old astronomer's globe. Below and to either side float two large hands made of overlapping dark iron blades, with no arms, held as if tending the fire. Twelve long ragged ash-grey streamers hang from under the sphere down to a low three-legged black iron brazier bowl on the ground, cold and empty. Four small old glass-and-iron fair lanterns, each with a small warm amber flame inside, hang on thin chains from the gold rings, two on each side.

SIZE. The whole thing is about eight metres tall and five wide; the brazier bowl would come up to a person's chest.

COLOURS. Eclipse black, old gold iron, deep crimson, pale rose-white, ash grey. The only warm amber is the four small lanterns. Nothing green and nothing blue.

STYLE. A realistic, richly painted picture with believable metal, cloth and fire, in a dark, beautiful fairy-tale mood. Elegant and solemn, not horror: no gore, no face, no eyes. Bold simple shapes with a strong silhouette and strong contrast of light and dark, and little fine surface texture.

BACKDROP AND LIGHT. A flat dark plum-grey backdrop, colour #383043, with no scenery, no floor pattern and no cast shadows on the backdrop. Even, soft light, so every part can be seen; the glow is its own.

LEAVE OUT. People, hands of people, creatures, lettering, labels, numbers, arrows, borders and frames.
```

## B2  The keeper who stayed (another way: the keeper as a whole figure)

Shape: 3:2, 1536 x 1024 or larger.  Save as: `keeper-sheet.png`.

```
Model sheet of a single towering fantasy boss for my role-playing game, Moon Faire, full figure, shown three times side by side on one sheet: front view, three-quarter view, back view. Each view is the same size and stands on the same ground line.

WHAT IT IS. The last keeper of a dead airship beacon, who swore its light would never go out and kept the promise long past his own death. A very tall, stooped figure, about seven metres tall, in a long heavy coat woven of black-green bramble, with small iron charms hanging from the thorns. Where his chest should be there is a rusted six-sided iron lantern cage, and inside it burns one deep crimson fire with a hundred tiny warm amber flames crowded round it. His head is bowed under a deep hood of the same bramble, with no face to be seen, only dark. One long thin hand of dark iron holds a tall hooked lamp-lighter's pole like a staff; the other hangs open at his side. The coat's hem breaks into thorny runners that trail on the ground.

COLOURS. Rusted iron, black-green thorn, deep crimson, pale cream. The only warm amber is the tiny flames in the cage. Nothing bright green and nothing blue.

STYLE. A realistic, richly painted picture with believable iron, thorn and fire, in a dark, beautiful fairy-tale mood. Sad and patient, not horror: no gore, no face, no eyes. Bold simple shapes with a strong silhouette and strong contrast of light and dark, and little fine surface texture.

BACKDROP AND LIGHT. A flat dark plum-grey backdrop, colour #383043, with no scenery, no floor pattern and no cast shadows on the backdrop. Even, soft light, so every part can be seen; the glow is its own.

## B3  The vigil (another way: a pale figure of candle wax)

Shape: 3:2, 1536 x 1024 or larger.  Save as: `vigil-sheet.png`.

```
Model sheet of a single towering fantasy boss for my role-playing game, Moon Faire, full figure, shown three times side by side on one sheet: front view, three-quarter view, back view. Each view is the same size and stands on the same ground line.

WHAT IT IS. Every candle ever lit on the moor to bring a dead beacon back, melted and poured into one tall veiled shape that keeps a vigil without end. A towering figure about seven and a half metres tall made of smooth pale bone-coloured candle wax: a tall column of a body heavy with long drips, hovering just above the ground. A smooth wax mask with no features for a face, behind a long smoke-grey veil that falls to the shoulders. Two long thin dripping arms with a candle wick at each fingertip. A crown of thirteen guttered candles: twelve dark, one burning deep crimson. Deep crimson light glows through the wax from inside, brightest along the drips.

COLOURS. Bone-pale wax, soot grey, deep crimson, pale rose-white. Nothing green, nothing blue, and no warm orange.

STYLE. A realistic, richly painted picture with believable wax, cloth and fire, in a dark, beautiful fairy-tale mood. Beautiful and eerie, not horror: no gore, no eyes. Bold simple shapes with a strong silhouette and strong contrast of light and dark, and little fine surface texture.

BACKDROP AND LIGHT. A flat dark plum-grey backdrop, colour #383043, with no scenery, no floor pattern and no cast shadows on the backdrop. Even, soft light, so every part can be seen; the glow is its own.

LEAVE OUT. People, creatures, lettering, labels, numbers, arrows, borders and frames.
