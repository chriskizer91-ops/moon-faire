The final boss in two forms

This is the last fight as you chose it on November 15: helpers first and then alone, wraiths and brambles as the helpers, and the whole party back to full HP and MP when the boss falls the first time, with a harder second round. I played it with the eight testers, and the balance holds.

The fight as you chose it

  • Form one, the fire, whole. The great wraith's own numbers under new names, with four helpers: wraiths and brambles it has called. It shows no damage.
  • It falls, and the Curtain Call comes down. Every witch gets up with full HP and MP, and Io's Deity is ready again.
  • The final form, the fire, broken open. Alone, with 20% more Life than form one and blows 10% bigger, acting twice as often.
  • On hard, only an expert wins: 12% of the time with no finds and 65% with all 18. Without the refill it was 13% and 63%, so the balance is the same.

The top number is how often that player wins with no finds, and the number under it is with all 18. The party is level 20 and arrives fresh from a rest spot.

What the refill changes

  • Hard and crazy come out the same as the version with no refill. The stronger final form takes back what the refill gives.
  • Easy and medium are a touch kinder to a player who thinks. For her they are now about as hard as the great wraith's fight on its own (medium, focused player: 35%, against 18% with no refill).
  • Nobody is punished for spending everything in form one. There is nothing to save for, so the fight is as hard on the tenth try as on the first.
  • It is a longer fight. About 56 to 67 party turns in a win, against 41 to 46 with no refill.
  • The careless player does worse. On easy she wins 0.8% of the time, or 6.9% with every find. With no refill it was 3.5% and 13%. The final form alone is a long fight, and she does not last it.

What you decided

  • The full refill is in. When form one falls, the whole party is up with full HP and MP.
  • Why it happens: it comes from the Jester's magic and from what the boss is, a black orb inside light. Life and death are her thing, so beating the first form tops everybody off. The words for it are still to write.
  • If the party falls in the final form, it starts again from before the first form.
  • A rest spot stands just outside the place where the fight happens.

How we got here

Before you chose, I played three ways of building the fight. The short version:

  • The helpers are most of what makes the great wraith hard. Alone with its own numbers, an attentive player beats it 100% of the time on easy and 100% on medium. With the helpers she wins 80% and 3.1%.
  • One body alone in both forms could be made hard enough with more Life, but easy became a walkover (100% for an attentive player), the Fox could never cast Crazy Quilt, and an expert who saved MP for the second form won hard 76% of the time, up from 17%.
  • Herbs do not rescue the last fight. With 20 or 60 of each herb in the bag my testers did slightly worse, because eating one costs a turn. A cleverer herb habit might gain a little; I have not built one.
