Skin prompts

Moon Faire, November 13. One simple 3D person, several painted skins. 11 skins on 5 models, 22 pictures.

How to use these
  1. Unzip Moon_Faire_Skin_Starters.zip from the chat. It holds each model's grey guide, face-guide.png and style-reference.jpg.
  2. Each skin is two pictures. For the body sheet, start a new ChatGPT chat, attach the model's guide and the style reference, and paste the prompt. For the face, attach the face guide and paste the face prompt.
  3. Save each picture under the name shown and send them back in the chat. One skin is enough to start.

Start with the lamp-seller's own skin (lampseller-A): two pictures. If those two lay onto the model well, the rest are worth making. The people here are stand-ins written by Claude Opus 5.5 through your key, not Moon Faire lore; change any of them.
