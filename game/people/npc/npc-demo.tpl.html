Fair folk who talk

Moon Faire, November 13. The painted people, wired for the game: each one stands, blinks, fidgets, turns to face you, talks and plays emotes. Drag to look round them.

What is wired
  • Talking. While Talk is on, the mouth opens and shuts in a speech rhythm, the head moves and the hands help. There are three ways of talking (calm, lively, gruff); each person has their own.
  • Emotes. Every person can play every emote. The gold-edged buttons are the ones chosen as most like that person.
  • On their own. They blink, shift and look about when left alone, each at their own pace.
  • Facing. They turn to face a point, as they will turn to the party.
  • Walking, running, sitting. They walk or run to a point and sit on a seat the scene gives them (a plain crate here). These are the Jester's own walk, run and sit, which every person shares.
  • Lighter weights. Each person can be built with half or a quarter of the triangles. The painted skin lies on the lighter build as it is.
  • Flipbook look. The switch the witches have: flipbook colours and an inked outline. It starts off.

The moves were written by Claude Fable 5.2 through your key. The people, their names and their ways are stand-ins, not Moon Faire lore. No Claude session can measure speed on your laptop.
