People from a template

Moon Faire, November 13. The Jester's finished model is the template for a simple person. The first people in the menu wear your painted skins: one body sheet and one face picture each, made in ChatGPT and laid onto the model. Drag to turn them.

    What this says
    • The painted skin is what turns a simple model into a person. The same lamp-seller model is three different women, and the men now have men's faces.
    • A new person costs two ChatGPT pictures and about a dime of fitting. The model underneath is made once (38 cents with Opus 5.5 at high effort, $1.08 with Fable 5.2) and reused for every skin.
    • The same pictures sit on the Opus 5.5 models nearly as well as on the Fable 5.2 models they were painted for, so which model built the shapes matters less than the painting.
    • What still shows: hair is a flat-coloured shape around the face, shoulders are angular, and the body under a man's clothes is still the Jester's. The skins came out more lifelike than the witches' painted look, because ChatGPT did not have the style picture.
    • Each person is about 12 thousand triangles and 15 draws, about a ninth of the Jester walking the fair square on your laptop. No Claude session can measure speed on your laptop.
