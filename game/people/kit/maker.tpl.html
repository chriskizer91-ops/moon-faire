People from a template

A test for Moon Faire, November 13. The Jester's finished model is the template. A Claude model reads one line about a person and writes a small file: colours, skin, a repainted face, hair, a hat, clothes. The Jester's skeleton and all her moves are reused, so every person can already stand, turn and walk. The lamp-seller was made by hand; the rest were made through your API key.

    What this says
    • Opus 5.5 at high effort is the setting to use: about 38 cents and two minutes a person. Extra-high costs about $1.33 and seven minutes for little more.
    • What decides whether a person is good enough is mostly the design, not the price. A woman in long clothes (the lamp-seller, the fortune-teller) suits the template and holds up. A man does not yet, because every person has the Jester's body and the shape of her face.
    • Each person is about 12 thousand triangles and 15 draws. The Jester took 106 thousand when she walked the fair square on your laptop. No Claude session can measure speed on your laptop.
