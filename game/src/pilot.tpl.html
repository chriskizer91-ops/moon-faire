Walking

  • W A S D or the arrow keys to walk. Hold Shift to run.
  • Or click or tap the ground and she walks there.
  • E at the well or on the floor cloth.
  • She goes behind the well, the lantern posts and the stalls. The two lanes end where the next paintings will begin.

Fighting

  • Nothing is timed. On her turn, pick a move with the buttons or 1 to 4.
  • With three wisps, click a wisp's bar or press Tab to choose her target.
  • A wisp's bar shows its Life in red. Fate grows in from the other end each time it hits her. When they meet it is sealed, and Sealed Fate ends it.
  • The rules and numbers are your approved combat engine, unchanged.

What to judge

  • Does she look as she does in her own theatre? Turn The painting off to see her against plain plum at the same size.
  • Does she stand on the ground: her size, her shadow, the light on her?
  • The line under the picture gives pictures a second on this machine. Your laptop is the one that counts.
