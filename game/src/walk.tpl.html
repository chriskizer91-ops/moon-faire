Walk the demo

Tap to go on
Building the Jester and the nine places…

How to walk

  • Tap or click the ground and she walks there, round whatever is in the way.
  • Or W A S D or the arrow keys. Hold Shift to run.
  • Walk off a place by one of its ways out and the next place opens.
  • Near someone, press Do it (or E) to talk. Press it again for their next line.
  • Map (or M) shows the world map. Tap a pin to go straight there.

What is in

  • All nine painted places, joined by their ways out. The airship flight between the mooring field and the landing ledge shows its story picture.
  • The eleven fair folk where the map puts them, with their lines from the chosen story. The captain is a grey stand-in until his model is built.
  • The Jester at her Lightest weight, the one you chose for walking.
  • The three rest spots, as places to stand. Resting does nothing yet.

What is not, yet

  • Your traced walking paths. Until they arrive, each place uses its starting shapes, so she can stray onto things and may not go behind everything she should. /*TRACED*/
  • Fights, the things that can happen, the lamp count and the gates. Every way out is open.
  • The other three witches.
  • The line under the picture gives pictures a second on this machine. No Claude session can measure your laptop: that number on your own screen is the one that counts.
