## W2 The stall row and the sparring ring

Guide: `W2_guide.png`

```
One production background painting for my fantasy role-playing game, Moon Faire. The game draws realistic 3D characters on top of this painting, walking on its ground.

Output exactly ONE landscape image, 2:1 (twice as wide as it is tall), 2048 x 1024 pixels or larger. Not a collage or a contact sheet.

I am attaching three images.
- Image 1 (W2_guide.png) is the CAMERA AND LAYOUT GUIDE: a grey-box model of this exact scene, drawn from the exact camera the game uses. Keep its camera, its perspective and its layout. Everything stays where the guide puts it and the size the guide makes it. Take only layout, scale and the direction of the light from it. Do not copy its grey colours or its blocky shapes: paint real things in their places. The pale areas are the ground the characters walk on. The bright magenta figure is a measuring marker 1.70 m tall, the height of a person here. Use it for scale, then leave it out and paint the bare ground where it stands.
- Image 2 (style-fair-square.jpg) is the finished painting of another place in this same game, the fair square. Match its craft, its finish, its realism and the way its lanterns glow. Take nothing else from it: not its layout or its tents, and not its hour unless this scene is at dusk too.
- Image 3 (character-reference.jpg) is one of the characters who will stand in this scene, shown from the front and the back. It is there to show how realistic the characters are. Never paint her, or anyone, into the scene.

VIEW. As in Image 1: a high three-quarter view, looking down at about 24 degrees through a long lens, like the painted backgrounds of classic role-playing games. The perspective is flattened: things at the back are only a little smaller than things at the front, and upright things stay upright. Not isometric, not straight down.

STYLE. A realistic, richly detailed, hand-painted scene with believable scale and painterly but detailed surfaces: real canvas, rope, wood, stone, earth and grass. It has to sit behind realistic 3D characters, so paint it as real as Image 3. Not pixel art, not a cartoon, not anime, no drawn outlines, not game tiles.

LIGHT. The game's characters are always lit the same way, so every painting in this set is lit the same way:
- The main light is warm and soft. It comes from behind the viewer and from the right, a little under 45 degrees up. The sides of things that face the viewer and the right are the lit sides. Soft shadows fall away from the viewer and to the left, toward the upper left of the picture, about as long as the thing is tall. Image 1 shows this.
- The moon, Auros, is behind the scene in the upper left of the sky. It edges the upper-left outlines of things with cool, pale lilac light. It does not cast the main shadows.
- A cool blue-violet sky light fills the shadows from low on the left. Shadows are soft and never black.
- The ground is warm in colour: earth, dry grass and warm stone.
- Only lit lanterns, the moon and the sky are bright. Everything else is calm enough that a brightly dressed character stands out against it.

LEAVE OUT, because the game draws them live on top: people, animals and creatures of any kind; the magenta marker; fireflies, moths, drifting smoke, sparks, glows of magic, rain and lightning. No lettering anywhere: signs and banners are blank or patterned. No captions, labels, borders, UI, grid lines or vignette.

THE HOUR. It is dusk, as in Image 2: a deep blue-violet sky with a last low band of rose and amber, and the lanterns just lit.

GROUND THE CHARACTERS WALK ON. The pale ground in the guide is level, clear and easy to read in the finished painting. Nothing hangs over it or hides it: no roof, awning, banner, rope, branch or string of flags crosses walkable ground. Every way out of the scene that the guide shows stays open and visible. Keep the open ground empty except for what the guide shows.

THE SCENE: THE STALL ROW AND THE SPARRING RING AT DUSK.
The stall row of a travelling moon fair: a straight lane of trodden earth running left to right past awning stalls, opening midway on its far side onto a roped sparring ring of sawdust. It is dusk, the last blue light going, and the four ring lanterns are the brightest things in the scene.
- The ground: one flat, wide lane of pale trodden earth with scattered straw runs straight across the middle of the picture from the left edge to the right edge, clear and empty. Midway it opens on the far side through a short earth mouth into a square of pale golden sawdust, the sparring ring, also flat and empty.
- At the very back, three canvas fair tents in faded stripes stand side by side, a larger round one directly behind the ring, with two thin bare wooden flag poles rising between them.
- To the far left and far right, in front of the tents, stand two plank booths with their shutters closed for the night, dark and unlit.
- The ring: a square of sawdust with a tall wooden lamp post at each of its four corners, each carrying a lit lantern that glows warm amber. One thick hemp rope sags waist-high from corner post to corner post along the far side and the two flanks, held up at the middle of each of those sides by a short wooden stake. The near side has no rope and is left open as the ring's mouth onto the lane.
- Left of the ring, facing the lane, is a timber market stall with a striped cloth awning and an empty counter. Right of the ring is its twin with a different faded awning. Both stand back from the lane and neither awning reaches over it.
- At the left end of the row, beside the left stall, stands an oven cart: a squat domed brick-and-clay bread oven on a heavy wooden cart with an iron door facing the lane, a short tin chimney and a dull ember glow at the door.
- At the right end of the row stands a low wooden hand cart with two spoked wheels and a flat top under a cloth. Past it the stalls stop and dark open grass begins, the lane running on to the right edge.
- Along the near side of the lane is a strip of trampled dusk-dark grass with only low things on it: a couple of straw bales on the left, a low wooden hitching rail in the middle and a small stack of crates on the right, all below waist height.
- The top of the picture is a narrow band, as in the guide. A deep dusk sky, a shade darker than early evening, violet-blue fading to a thin band of dull rose at the horizon. Against it stand the peaked tops of the fair tents and the bare flag poles in dark silhouette, with a few first stars. Auros, the moon, hangs large, pale and nearly full in the upper left.
```
