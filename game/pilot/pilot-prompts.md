# Moon Faire pilot painting prompts

The text of the page `pilot-prompts.html`, which also holds the images to attach.

## How to run it

1. Look at the two labelled layouts below. If a layout is not what you want, tell me before you paint anything.
2. Save the four images to attach. They are on this page, and in the zip I sent in the chat.
3. Start a new ChatGPT chat for each painting. Attach its three images in the order given, then paste its prompt.
4. Make each one two to four times. Keep the one whose layout matches the guide best, even if another is prettier.
5. Save the keepers as W1.png and F1.png. A second keeper is W1b.png. Send them back as they come, with no squeezing.

## Images to attach

- **G1** `W1_guide.png`: Layout guide: the fair square. Attach first with prompt W1. Grey boxes drawn from the game's walking camera.
- **G2** `F1_guide.png`: Layout guide: the moor clearing. Attach first with prompt F1. Drawn from the game's fight camera.
- **P1** `P1_envoi_moor_painting.jpg`: Finish guide: your Envoi moor painting. Attach second with both prompts. It shows the craft and finish to match.
- **S1** `S1_style_jester_standing.jpg`: Realism guide: the Jester. Attach third with both prompts. It shows how real the characters are.

One production background painting for my fantasy role-playing game, Moon Faire. The game draws realistic 3D characters on top of this painting, walking on its ground.

Output exactly ONE landscape image, 2:1 (twice as wide as it is tall), 2048 x 1024 pixels or larger. Not a collage or a contact sheet.

I am attaching three images.
- Image 1 is the CAMERA AND LAYOUT GUIDE: a grey-box model of this exact scene, drawn from the exact camera the game uses. Keep its camera, its perspective and its layout. Every tent, stall, lantern post, the well, the round square and the two lanes stay where the guide puts them and the size the guide makes them. Take only layout, scale and the direction of the light from it. Do not copy its grey colours or its blocky shapes: paint real things in their places. The bright magenta figure is a measuring marker 1.70 m tall, the height of a person here. Use it for scale, then leave it out and paint the bare ground where it stands.
- Image 2 is a finished background painting from another game of mine. Match its craft and finish. Take nothing else from it: not its place, not its camera, not its night colours.
- Image 3 is one of the characters who will stand in this scene, shown from the front and the back. It is there to show how realistic the characters are. Never paint her, or anyone, into the scene.

VIEW. As in Image 1: a high three-quarter view, looking down at about 24 degrees through a long lens, like the painted backgrounds of classic role-playing games. The perspective is flattened: things at the back are only a little smaller than things at the front, and upright things stay upright. Not isometric, not straight down.

STYLE. A realistic, richly detailed, hand-painted scene with believable scale and painterly but detailed surfaces: real canvas, rope, wood, stone, earth and grass. It has to sit behind realistic 3D characters, so paint it as real as Image 3. Not pixel art, not a cartoon, not anime, no drawn outlines, not game tiles.

LIGHT. The game's characters are always lit the same way, so every painting in this set is lit the same way:
- The main light is warm and soft. It comes from behind the viewer and from the right, a little under 45 degrees up. The sides of things that face the viewer and the right are the lit sides. Soft shadows fall away from the viewer and to the left, toward the upper left of the picture, about as long as the thing is tall. Image 1 shows this.
- The moon, Auros, is behind the scene in the upper left of the sky. It edges the upper-left outlines of things with cool, pale lilac light. It does not cast the main shadows.
- A cool blue-violet sky light fills the shadows from low on the left. Shadows are soft and never black.
- The ground is warm in colour: earth, dry grass and warm stone.
- Only lit lanterns, the moon and the sky are bright. Everything else is calm enough that a brightly dressed character stands out against it.

LEAVE OUT, because the game draws them live on top: people, animals and creatures of any kind; the magenta marker; fireflies, moths, drifting smoke, sparks, glows of magic, rain and lightning. No lettering anywhere: signs and banners are blank or patterned. No captions, labels, borders, UI, grid lines or vignette.

GROUND THE CHARACTERS WALK ON. The round square, both lanes and the open ground between the tents are level, clear and easy to read. Nothing hangs over them or hides them: no roof, awning, banner, rope, branch or string of flags crosses walkable ground. Do not string bunting or flags across the square: the game adds its own. Every tent door and every stall front that faces the square is fully visible. Keep the middle of the square empty except for what the guide shows.

- The ground: a round square about 14 m across of pale, trodden earth, edged by a ring of worn flat stones. A trodden earth lane, about 4 m wide, leaves the square to the left edge of the picture, and another to the right edge.
- In the square, toward the back: a performer's floor cloth lying flat on the ground, a rug about 4.5 m by 3 m in deep red with a gold border, with four small brass footlights along each short side. It is completely flat: no stage and no platform.
- In the square, front right of the middle: a small round stone well with a low wall, two wooden posts, a little shingled roof and a bucket.
- Eight lantern posts round the square, exactly where the guide puts them: dark wooden posts 3.3 m tall, each with one lit lantern of warm amber glass that throws a small warm pool of light on the ground.
- Tents and stalls where the guide's boxes stand. Back left, a round tent in broad wine-red and cream stripes with a pointed roof. Back middle, two open market stalls with cloth awnings and wooden counters. Back right, a square tent of indigo cloth with a pale star pattern. On the left, above the lane, a square tent of ochre and rust cloth; below the lane, an open stall. On the right, above the lane, an open stall; below the lane, a round tent of green and gold cloth. Each tent has its door flap toward the square, and each stall its counter. Their colours are rich but softened by the dusk.
- At the bottom corners of the picture, the tops of two more tents frame the scene, as in the guide.
