Moon Faire: the fight on its own

The party of four against the foes, on the five painted fight places, with the approved combat rules. This page only asks for a fight and shows what came back, the same way the open world will.

What came back

Nothing yet. Start a fight.

How to play

When it is a witch's turn, her moves are the buttons under the picture. A move aimed at a foe goes to the foe whose bar is lit: tap another foe's bar to aim there. Mend and Revive ask which friend. The strip along the top is who acts next; a foe's Heavy, Crush or Eclipse is named there before it comes. The camera, the speed and the Flipbook look can be changed during the fight.
