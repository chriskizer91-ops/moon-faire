Where he is: first build

  • You said he looks pretty good and not to remake him from scratch, so everything else is as it was.
  • His head is now the head the witches wear (the Jester's face model, at a man's size, with a wider jaw and a lower crown), with his own face from C1 painted on it and his profile from C3 at the sides. Press "Blank doll" to see the shape with no paint.

  • His body is measured from B1 and B2 and wears those paintings, laid on from four sides. His sleeves take their sides from A3.
  • His hands are the full hand model the witches use, at a man's size, in two plain colours: glove leather and skin.
  • He stands 1.78 m in his boots. That is my number for a man of average height; nothing in the paintings fixes it.

The gold on his boots

  • Flecks sit on the leather of each boot and move with it. Each one glints in its own time.
  • Motes drift slowly round his feet while he stands.
  • The trail: a boot sheds dust whenever it moves, and the dust hangs where it was shed and fades. Here he walks on the spot, so the dust streams away behind him. In a game it will be left along his path.

Rough, or not built

  • His neck is one plain skin colour, with no painting on it.
  • His hair reads as a hood: one smooth shell with a hard edge, and patches of the wrong colour where it turns in beside his neck.
  • His body is his clothes as one smooth skin, not a body with clothes on it. The chest ruffles, lace, corset straps, buckles, trouser holes and boot straps are paint, not shapes, and the paint is soft up close. Only the sleeve ruffles are real shapes.
  • Under his arms and between his legs the paint is drawn out, because no painting looks there.
  • His face does not blink or talk yet (C2 is not used yet). His hand paintings (D) and boot paintings (E) are not used yet either.
  • Not built: the hat, the belt and its ring, the pouch, the scroll, the charms, the dagger, the pearls and the feather earring. Rounds 1 and 2 of your paintings for all of these are saved.
  • The walk and the talk are first drafts borrowed from the Ranger's stride. Nothing bends as cloth. I cannot watch movement play here, so its timing needs your eye.
