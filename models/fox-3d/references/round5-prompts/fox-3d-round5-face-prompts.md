Written by Claude, 12 Nov 2026, after Chris said: the Jester's face looks good on her but "it's still the Jester"; the four are unique characters; he wants the Fox to look like she does in round 4 and suggested another set of face images.

## Why

- She now has the Jester's 3D head with **her own painted face** on it, so she is no longer the Jester. That face comes from sheet C3, where it is small (about 107 pixels between her pupils), so it is soft close up.
- These three pictures give her a face as large and sharp as the Jester's, looking the way she does in round 4. They are framed so I can lay them on the head by the eyes and mouth without stretching the rest.
- Order: F1 first, until it is her. Then F2 and F3, each with F1 attached. No teeth in any of them.

## What I do with them

- F1 becomes her face picture, laid on the head by pupils and lip line (the head's own frame: eyes at 1.5246, lip line 0.066 below). Pupils are read off a gridded copy by eye, never found automatically.

- F3's side view is the check for the head's shape, and gives the paint for cheek and jaw at the sides.
