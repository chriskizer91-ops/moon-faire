Fox Witch 3D

The Fox of the Moon Faire in 3D. In progress: the Fox in her corset, patchwork trousers, boots and bracelets, with her own face on her own head shape, and her belt, pouch and shears. Her hair and bracelets swing, and the flounces on her trousers are real cloth. Her skin is one tone from face to hands. Her head is 10% larger and her face is reshaped in depth, as chosen on November 13. Her moves are her own talk, laugh, dance, curtsey and pounce, with the Jester’s bow, twirl, wave and jump. Her shoulders now go with her arms, and her wrists and fingers trail and settle. Her shears come out of their sheath into her hands, and she has three spells: Hemline Fangs, Blue Mischief and Borrowed Face.

Drag to turn her; pinch or scroll to zoom. Walk around puts a round pad on the stage: drag it and she walks the way you push; push to the edge and she runs. On a keyboard use W A S D or the arrow keys, Shift to run, Space to jump. Body is how she holds herself: it stays until you pick another. Her moves play once over whatever she is doing, hovering included. Half speed slows a move down. Shears has her take her shears out, snip them, part them into her two daggers and put them away. Her spells so far are Hemline Fangs, Blue Mischief and Borrowed Face. Flipbook look redraws her like her pixel flipbook; it starts off. What you see under her bodice is charcoal leggings, as in painting B4: that is her body before the trousers go on.
