Her hands and arms now

  • Her left hand and forearm are painted from D1, her right from D2. Nothing is mirrored, so each has its own lines and moles.
  • Her nails are the ones you painted: short and natural, with a clear gloss over them.
  • The paint runs from her fingertips to about 15 cm above the wrist. Above that her skin carries on in the same tone and grain, up to her shoulder.
  • Her wrist is slimmer: 4.7 cm across, as D1 and D2 show it against her hand. It was 5.3 cm. Say if you want the old wrist back.
  • The shape of her hands was not changed.

Rough, or not here yet

  • Your painted hand has a longer palm and shorter fingers than the hand shape she wears, which is the Jester's. The paint is fitted to that shape, so the paint on her fingers is drawn out a little.
  • The thin edge of the hand, on the little-finger side and round the base of the thumb, is soft paint with no detail: neither painting shows it.
  • Where her arm meets her shoulder there is still a step. That is the shape of her body, which I have not touched.
  • Her fingers do not bend yet. That comes with movement.
  • Her hair is still painted from the old pictures, and the fine flyaway wisps are missing.
  • Rings, bracelets, earrings and necklaces.
  • Belts, satchel, pouch, charms, the strap hooks as real objects.
  • Sleeves and the headband tails as hanging cloth. For now they are stiff.
  • Bow, sword, knives, and all movement.
