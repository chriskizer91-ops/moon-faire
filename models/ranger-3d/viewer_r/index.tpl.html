New in stage two

  • Her face: your painting C1, on a head shaped from her profile in C3. From brow to chin the middle line of her face is within half a millimetre of the painted profile.
  • Ears, shaped and painted from the profile.
  • Hair: one form that swells into the bun, wearing four of your paintings (side, front, back and top), with a few loose locks.
  • Headband with its knot and two tails, the turquoise in its gold setting, and the drop on her forehead.
  • Glasses: thin wire and lilac lenses, sized from C2. There is a button to take them off.
  • Wrists: the forearm now narrows and runs into the hand.

Rough, or not here yet

  • Her face does not move yet: no blink and no change of expression.
  • The fine flyaway wisps round her hair are missing, and the loose locks are simple.
  • Earrings and necklaces, with the rest of her jewellery.
  • Painted detail on her arms and hands.
  • Belts, satchel, pouch, charms, bracelets, the strap hooks as real objects.
  • Sleeves and the headband tails as hanging cloth. For now they are stiff.
  • Bow, sword, knives, and all movement.
