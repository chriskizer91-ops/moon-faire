New: charms, swinging bags, shoulders

  • Six charms, built from your charm sheet: three turquoise crystals under the satchel, the bead string from the ring buckle, the cluster of gold balls from the square buckle, and the amber string under the pouch. Each has its clasp, chain, beads and cut stone, at the lengths in your front painting.
  • The bags are no longer glued on. Each hangs from its belt on a hinge, and each charm from its clasp. "Give them a swing" pushes them; they swing and settle back against her.
  • Her thigh now pushes a bag outward instead of passing through it. The new pose "Legs apart" shows it.
  • The strap hooks now move with her skin, so the outer curls ride her shoulder when she lifts her arms.
  • The straps are redrawn cleanly over the top of each shoulder, front to back, where they used to blur.

Her belts, bags and strap hooks

  • Two studded belts, each a real loop tilted round her hips, crossing front and back. The ring buckle is at her front right; the square buckle and blue keeper at her front left. 2.4 cm wide, with tucked ends and punched holes.
  • The satchel hangs by two loops on her right hip, built to its own painting. 20 cm across, 19 tall. The pouch hangs behind her left hip. 9 cm across, 14 tall.
  • Four strap hooks, front and back: a brass buckle with a black iron curl either side.
  • A choice I made: your paintings draw each bag face-on in every view, which one bag cannot do. The satchel hangs on the side of her right hip turned a little forward, so it shows from the front, the side and the back. Say if you would rather have it further round to the front.

Her hair

  • The swell over her forehead stood 2.5 to 4.6 cm too high just behind the band. It is lowered to the slope in your side painting (within 3 mm). Her crown, bun, band and face are untouched.

Her boots

  • Laid over your side, front, back and sole paintings at one scale and corrected until the outlines agreed to within a few millimetres.
  • Slimmer: the sole is 9.6 cm across at the ball, where it was 12 cm, and the shaft and toe are narrower to match.
  • The sock is lower and smaller: three soft rolls ending 29 cm up, where it stood 32 cm.
  • The lacing follows your painting: nine pairs of brass eyelets set back from the front edge and running down onto the instep, laces crossing over the tongue, a bow with hanging tips.
  • Seams as painted: the toe cap, the piece the eyelets sit in, the seam down to the welt, a domed heel counter, a strap up the back with a pull loop.
  • Worn charcoal-brown leather, a dark warm-grey sole with bold tan stitching round the welt, eight lugs a side.

Rough, or not here yet

  • The leather is one worn texture all over. Your paintings rub paler on the toe cap and heel; hers does not yet.
  • The shaft is smooth: the soft creases at the ankle are missing.
  • Under the sole the tread is simple bars, not the crosses in E7.
  • From earlier stages: the paint on her fingers is drawn out a little; the edge of the hand is soft paint; a step where her arm meets her shoulder; her hair is still painted from the old pictures.
  • Rings, bracelets, earrings and necklaces.
  • Each charm is one stiff piece on a hinge: its chain does not bend, and charms can pass through each other.
  • The gold caps on the stones are plain, without the leaf work in your charm sheet.
  • She does not walk yet, so things only swing when you press the button. Once she moves they will swing with her.
  • With her arms straight out the iron curls bend a little with her skin, as if soft.
  • Sleeves and the headband tails as hanging cloth. For now they are stiff.
  • Bow, sword, knives, and all movement.
