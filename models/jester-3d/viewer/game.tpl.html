Drag to turn the view; pinch or scroll to zoom. The round pad walks her, and pushing it to the edge makes her run. Body is how she holds herself: it stays until you pick another. Moves and Spells in the game play once over whatever she is doing, so she can cast from her knees or juggle sitting down. She casts toward her own left. This page has only the five spells the game gives her. Her other spells are still in the model, on the night square page.

The fight. The two sacks stand in for foes: one with a knight's numbers (1,000 Life) and one with a wisp's (300). Each has one bar. The red part is the Life it has left, and its Fate grows over that from the other end. A foe hits her lands a blow: she laughs it off, and that foe gains 500 Fate. A heavy blow gives 1,000. A friend in peril gives every foe a little, three times at most. You can see Fate in the square too: dust falls round the foe like sand and thickens as it grows, and the dust round her follows the foe nearest its seal. When Fate covers all the Life a foe has left, it is sealed: its dust locks into a ring and a small eclipse hangs over it.

Her spells. Gilded Rebuke does 40 to her target. The Attack taunt does 50, and three small moons circle her: the next three blows she will catch. One goes out with each blow, and they all go when she makes her next move. The Heal taunt costs 10 MP, heals her 120, and brings the three moons. Sealed Fate goes to a sealed foe and ends it, and every other foe loses a fifth of the ended foe's full Life. When that leaves no foe standing, the same cast runs on into Curtain Call, and three lights stand up behind her where her fainted friends lay. The buttons always work here, so you can watch any move at any time: Sealed Fate seals a foe first if it was not sealed, and Curtain Call takes every foe. New foes are stood up a few seconds after the last one goes.

Switches: Flipbook look redraws her at the sprites' pixel size in her own 56 colours. Glow lets bright things shine; turn it off if the page runs slowly. Night square puts her back in the little theatre when it is off. Keys: 1 to 5 cast her spells, H is a blow, G a heavy blow, P a friend in peril, Space jumps.
