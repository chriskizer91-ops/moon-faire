Drag to turn her; pinch or scroll to zoom. Body is how she holds herself: it stays until you pick another. Moves and Spells play once over whatever she is doing, so she can talk while she sits, or cast from her knees. The stool is conjured when she sits and goes when she stands. The first six spells follow her flipbook, and the small picture shows her flipbook frame for the same moment; Ill Omen, Stop, Royal Reversal and The Last Toll are built from the move sheet and have no drawn frames yet. As in the sprites she casts toward her left, where the target stands at screen right. Flipbook look redraws the model at the sprites' pixel size in her own 56 colours.
