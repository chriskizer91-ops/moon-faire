# Jester game model: reference image prompts

Written November 11, 2026, for Chris to run in an image generator.

## What these are for

A new 3D Jester built as a game character: one model with a skeleton that can walk, run, squat, kneel and cast in a 3D world, seen from every side, including walking toward and away from the camera. It replaces the flipbook character of about 400 images. The look to match is Chris's three detailed pixel sheets (the 20-pose sheet, the 20-figure sheet with back views, and the 11-figure sheet), not the small 128-pixel sprites and not the HD paintings.

The sheets show her in action poses with cloth flying. To build and paint a model I need her standing still, from exact angles, with and without the veil, plus close views of the parts that are tiny in the sheets.

## How to run them

1. Start a new chat in the image generator. Attach the three sheets.
2. Paste **Standing rules** below as the first message.
3. Paste one prompt at a time. One prompt makes one image.
4. If a result breaks a rule, reply "Redo" and say what to fix. Use the checklist at the end.
5. Save each image under its prompt's code: `A1.png`, `B2.png`. For a second keeper of the same prompt, `A1b.png`.
6. If the chat gets long and she starts to drift, start a new chat, attach the three sheets again and paste the standing rules again.
7. Zip them and send them to me in the chat.

**Order of importance:** sets A, B and C are what I need to start. D, E and F can come later.

## Standing rules (paste first, with the three sheets attached)

```
The three attached sheets show my character, the Jester Witch. I need reference drawings of her for a 3D modeller. These rules apply to every image in this chat:

1. Draw the same character in the same pixel-art style, colours and amount of detail as the attached sheets. Draw her larger, not more detailed and not more realistic.
2. Her costume stays exactly as in the sheets: black jester cap with two large curved horns, each ending in a big black pompom; black ruff collar; teal pendant; purple bodice; black belt with buckle, pouch and hanging gold bells; sleeves ending in big black ruffled cuffs; layered ragged skirt in black and olive, open at the front; purple leggings with a darker star pattern; white striped socks above chunky black lace-up platform boots; a sheer wine-red and gold veil that hangs from the cap over her shoulders and arms down to her ankles, with gold dotted edges and ragged points; thin ribbon strands hanging from the cap beside her face.
3. Her costume is not symmetrical. Keep the pouch and the gold bells on the same sides as in the sheets. Never mirror her.
4. The forehead of the cap is plain black: no cross, no stitch mark, no symbol.
5. Young, smooth hands. No veins or tendons.
6. Flat, straight-on view like a blueprint: no perspective, camera level at her waist, not looking up or down at her.
7. Flat, even lighting. No shadow on the ground, no glow, no sparkles and no spell effects unless I ask for them.
8. Plain flat mid-grey background (#808080) and nothing else in the picture: no text, numbers, labels, borders, grid or floor.
9. Every figure fully inside the frame with clear grey space on all sides and between figures. Nothing cut off, nothing overlapping.
10. When an image has more than one drawing of her, they are all the same size, with their feet on the same line.

"Standing pose" means: standing straight, feet a little apart, toes forward, arms straight and held about 30 degrees away from her sides, hands open and relaxed with fingers slightly apart, head level, calm face, eyes open, looking straight ahead. Cloth hangs still, as if there is no wind and she is not moving.

Reply "ready" and wait for my first request.
```

## Checklist before keeping an image

- The cap's forehead is plain: no cross.
- Both horns and both pompoms are there and fully in frame.
- Pouch and bells are on the same sides as in the sheets.
- Boots and hem are not cut off at the bottom.
- The background is flat grey (or flat black for set F), with no floor shadow.
- No text, numbers or labels.
- She looks like the sheets: same pixel style, not smoother or more realistic.
- In two-figure images, both figures are the same size.
