New in this stage

  • Ankles: when she walks, her sole lies flat on the ground until her leg is too far back, then her heel lifts. In the air her foot hangs loose with the toes down, and comes up to land on the heel. Her ankle can no longer bend further than a real one, and her stiff boots no longer fold at the toe.
  • Her knee starts to give as her foot leaves the ground.
  • Wrists: when her hand turns over, the turn now runs along her forearm instead of twisting at the wrist. Her wrist can no longer bend further than a real one.
  • The wave is a small, coy one now: her hand at chest height, her elbow down by her side, her head tipped toward it. It comes from her elbow, with her hand a moment behind.

From the earlier stages

  • Her belt, pouch, crystal, necklaces and bracelets (stage five). Her cloak with the moss, and her painted hands (stage four). Her arms, her moving hair and skirt, and her first moves (stage three). Her face, glasses, hair and hat (stage two). Her body, bodice and skirt (stage one).

What is rough, and what is not here yet

  • The cloak is a first build. Its cloth and trim are drawn by code in F6's colours, not cut from your swatches yet. It is not see-through.
  • Its body is fuller and rounder than in your picture, because it rides on the skirt. Its sleeves are narrower than the wide wings in your picture.
  • Hair and sleeves can pass through each other at her shoulders. A forearm can show through a sleeve when her elbow bends.
  • I tuned the moves from still pictures and cannot watch them play.
  • The thin edge of each hand (thumb side, little-finger side) is soft paint with no detail: the paintings do not show it.
  • The new pieces are plain shapes: the belt has no gold vine or holes yet, the pouch has no flap, studs or filigree, and the necklace pendant does not swing.
  • Not here yet: her own boots, book, blades, and her spells.
