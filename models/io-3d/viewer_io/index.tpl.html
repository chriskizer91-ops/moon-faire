New in this stage

  • Her face: painting C1 on a head shaped to her profile in C3 (within a millimetre from chin to forehead), with her ears and neck. No hair is painted on it.
  • She blinks, and she talks: her eyes and mouths are the ones you painted in C9.
  • Glasses: round lenses in dark bronze wire.
  • Hair: about 120 wavy tresses in her own browns, from under the hat to below her hips. Her fringe is real hair too: 24 locks that sweep out from her parting across the top of her forehead and down the sides of her face, so it frames her face and joins the rest of her hair.
  • Hat: the wide floppy brim, the lace band with its chain, three crosses, the ringed cross and two horns, and the crown slumping to her right. Measured off C4 and C5; the felt, lace and horn are your swatches in F9.

From stage one

  • Body, bodice, skirt, bare arms and legs, as you approved them. The dark patch and purple streaks on her shoulders are gone, and the bodice straps now run over them.

What is rough, and what is not here yet

  • Her hair is stiff: it does not move or part round her arms yet. Up close a tress reads as a ribbon, and the ends are blunt.
  • The top of her head under the hat is bare. Her brows do not move, and the emotions in C10 are not in yet.
  • The crown of the hat has soft creases only, not the deep folds of the paintings.
  • Still stand-ins: the stiff skirt, the boots, plain skin on arms, legs and hands.
  • Not here yet: robe, belt, pouch, crystal, necklaces, bracelets, book, blades, and all movement.
