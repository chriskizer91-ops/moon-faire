What she is

  • Io's body, measured from her paintings, as one skin with no joins from her neck to her ankles. The waist and hips follow B5, where she wears only shorts, so they are a little fuller than under her laced bodice.
  • Her own face, blinking and talking, her six feelings, her painted hands and arms, and her long wavy hair, now with a real crown and a centre parting (C11, C12) where the hat used to be.
  • Bare feet, refitted to her five foot paintings (FT1 to FT5): their outline from below, their height along the instep from both sides, a round heel, a lower outer edge, and wider toes that sit side by side as FT3 shows them. Her toes bend at their joints when her heel lifts or she kneels.
  • Her feet are painted from those five paintings: each one laid onto the part of the foot that faces its way (top, sole, both sides, back, front). The toenails are the painted nails, with a light gloss.
  • Her legs are repainted from LG1 (front, side and back, from the shorts' hem to her ankles), with each painting's kneecap and hem put at hers. Her bare middle is repainted from TO3 and TO2: the navel and the skin around it at their true size.
  • The sports bra and bike shorts are real clothes, not paint: each is made from her own body, a couple of millimetres thicker, so it fits her exactly and moves exactly as she does. Every close-fitting piece in the wardrobe can be made the same way.
  • The shorts come down to mid-thigh, ten centimetres above her knee. B5's shorts are shorter; say if you want those instead.
  • Flipbook look: under Show. Pixel size slides from the flipbook's own (4.75 mm of her to a pixel) down to 0.8 mm, and starts at 1.2 mm. Colours slides from smooth to the flipbook's own. Eight of her skin tones are added to the flipbook's colours on this page.

What is rough

  • TO1 shows a slimmer figure than Io's, so I kept her body's shape and used the new paintings only for her skin. Her arms and hands (AR1, AR2, HN1 to HN3) are not repainted yet.
  • Seen from the side her smaller toes look a little long and flat; the paintings show them more curled.
  • From the side, the top of her forehead (under where the hat was) is a little lighter than the rest of her face.
  • The hair on the top of her head is new and is a first pass; the long hair below is Io's as approved.
  • I checked her from stills only. I could not watch her moves play.
