What this page is

  • Only the light models. Each outfit is one model of about 50,000 triangles (the full outfits are 450,000 to 560,000): her body, the clothes and the bun as one mesh, with the paintings, shine and sheen baked into its pictures.
  • Her face is its own small live mesh, so she still blinks, talks and shows her feelings. Every move works, and skirts, capes and sleeves still swing.
  • The fae's wings, the bard's feather and the masquerade mask stay real see-through pieces (they count inside the 50,000).
  • Each outfit downloads only its light model (about 3 MB) and the small file of how it moves, the first time you pick it. The full outfits' paintings are never loaded and the full clothes are never drawn.

What is different from the full doll

  • The look is baked, so it does not change with the light the way the full model's does: velvet sheen and shine are close copies, not live.
  • She always wears the bun, and there is no bra-and-shorts look here (that has no light model).
