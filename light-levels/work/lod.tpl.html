What one of her costs at each level

How to read this. All four shows the same witch four times, doing the same thing through the same camera. Fight distance pulls back to about the size she would be with a whole fight on screen; Face goes right in. Tap one level to see it alone and full size; flip between two to spot what changed. The number at the bottom of the picture is how many pictures a second this device is drawing right now.

The fight test is the real question: four witches at once (four Jesters stand in for the four), in the night square, with a crowd of foes. They take turns casting; a spell chip makes the next one cast it. The foes are plain hooded stand-ins of about 2,000 triangles each, so real foes will cost more than these. Try Four at Full last: it is the heavy one, and a phone may refuse it.

What each level gives up.
  • Full is the model as it is on her other pages.
  • Trim has half the rings in every part, a face and cuffs thinned by half again, coarser cloth, and no boot stitches, eyelets or lace tips.
  • Light thins every part again and works out a third as many points of cloth, half as often.
  • Lightest keeps her own painted face, hands, bodice and leggings. Every other part gives up its own weave and shares one of three plain materials: matte, glossy or metal. Her cloth has a quarter of the points and no fine pleats, and only her big parts throw a shadow.

Nothing about how she moves changes between levels: the same skeleton, the same moves and the same spell effects. The cloth is still worked out as cloth at every level, with fewer points. Switches: Flipbook look redraws her at the sprites' pixel size in her own colours; with it on, two sliders appear: Pixel size opens at 1.2 mm of her to a pixel and goes from the flipbook's own size down to as fine as this screen, and Colours goes from smooth to the flipbook's own, so anything between the look and no look can be tried; Glow lets bright things shine; Shadows turns her real shadow on and off.
