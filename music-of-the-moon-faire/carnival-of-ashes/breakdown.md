## How it fits Moon Faire

- Key: G minor is B-flat major's dark twin, the family that docs/music-requests/suno-cues.md asks for. It can sit next to Moonlit Forest Path, or any later piece in that family, with no drone between them. Herbal Decay (E minor) is outside the family, so give that change a gap.
- It is close to the fight the music requests ask for (piece 3: G minor, about 132 beats a minute, a sudden stop, a bare moment, a big return). It also has the mood of the Jester's Waltz (piece 1), in the minor: a waltz that opens on a music-box tune. It could be her dark waltz, the fight, or both.
- Cut within one part, or between parts that sit next to each other. The steadiest stretch is 0:17 to 1:19. Because the song slows down later, joining something early to something late will show as a jump in speed.
- The full-band parts are dense and loud, so small sounds get buried under them. The opening, the lead-in, the quiet break and the ending leave room for her bells, her steps and the wisps.
- It fades out on the plucked tune rather than stopping dead. For a hard stop, end at 2:16.75, the last bar of the last waltz, with a short fade.
