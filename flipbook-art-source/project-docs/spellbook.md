Six spells for each of the four, with MP costs and a description of what each looks like, written so an image generator can draw it. Every look follows its caster's moon phase and colours.

## How to draw any spell

Every spell image follows the same rules, so all four casters look like they belong to one game.

| Caster | Colours | Shapes | Signature mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ranger | Silver-white, pale moon blue, a touch of her corset's royal blue | Straight lines, arrows, perfect circles, clean lens flares | A full-moon disc that flashes behind every spell |
| Herbal Witch | Leaf green, pollen gold, wine magenta from her hat | Vines, petals, curling tendrils, crescents | A small waxing crescent that grows fuller as the spell lands |
| Fox | Cold blue-white foxfire, plus patchwork reds, teals and purples | Flickering wisps, stitched seams, swirls, fox-tail silhouettes | Her collar bell, drawn as a ring of sound; silent on her strongest spells |
| Jester | Violet and magenta sigils, ink black, gold from her bells | Rune rings, countdown numerals, theatre curtains, playing-card shapes | A black new-moon disc rimmed with a thin violet edge |

Base prompt for any spell image (add the spell's look after it):

```
Pixel art game spell effect, 16-bit JRPG HD-2D style matching the attached character sheet. Full-body character casting on the left third, the effect reaching across the rest of the frame. Flat backdrop #383043, soft ground shadow, crisp pixels, no text, no border. Use only the caster's colours and shapes from the spellbook rules.
```
