## Where these stories live

Every story is set on Thareia, on its moon, or on one of the other planets in the lore. Aethermoor is Thareia's known realm: airships lifted by amber sunstone, the Gloomfen marsh with Bogmire and Misthollow, the Aurosi shipwrights, and Ironhold.

The four characters come from real Renaissance Faire outfits. So the proposed hook is **the Moon Faire**: a festival airship that tours Aethermoor on sunstone lift. Its four performers are secretly its strongest casters, and each hides a weapon in her costume.

The moon is more than scenery. It is where all magic comes from (next section), so a later chapter can go there.

## The moon rule

All magic on Thareia is moonlight, and the less moon in the sky, the more of it a caster carries inside her. Each of the four draws on one phase, so the phases set the power order.

| Phase | Character | Magic | Power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full Moon | Ranger (blue princess) | Light | Steady, honest |
| Waxing Moon | Herbal Witch (horned hat) | Healing and summoning | Epic |
| Waning Crescent | Fox (bell collar) | Foxfire and mischief | Stronger than the Witch |
| New Moon | Jester (noble witch) | Fate | Power beyond power |

## The four

All four use magic, all four carry a hidden weapon, and each one nods to the moon.

### The Ranger: Full Moon

- **Magic:** moonlight anyone can see. Arrows of light, flares that reveal hidden enemies, barriers, blinding.
- **Hidden weapon:** a folding moonsteel bow pressed flat into the back of her corset. The beaded strands of her necklace are its spare bowstring.
- **Character:** the steadiest power in the party. The gem in her headband reads the light.

### The Herbal Witch: Waxing Moon

- **Magic:** waxing is the growing phase, so her magic grows things. She heals with herbs and draughts and reads remedies from her spellbook.
- **Summons:** at her peak she calls up great growing things, such as a guardian of root and bark or a moon-moth the size of a ship's sail.
- **Hidden weapon:** the crescent-moon pendant on her necklace unfolds into a golden harvest sickle.

### The Fox: Waning Crescent

- **Magic:** foxfire, a cold blue flame that burns only what she chooses. Also illusions and doubles, swapping an ally and an enemy into each other's places, and stealing buffs.
- **Character:** stronger than the Witch and knows it, which is why she plays. The bell on her collar is a vow that keeps her honest. When it goes silent, she has stopped playing.
- **Hidden weapon:** her dressmaker's shears split into twin daggers, and needles sewn into the patchwork skirt are throwing darts.

### The Jester: New Moon

- **Magic:** the dark moon nobody can see. She casts outcomes, not fire: Doom (a countdown to certain death), Stop (time halts for the target), Gravity (takes a fraction of an enemy's HP).
- **Character:** a licensed fool, the only one who may say true things to a thane's face. Her turquoise pendant holds the secrets she has been told.
- **Hidden weapon:** the gold bells on her hip are the head of a chain flail. Everyone hears her coming, and nobody takes a jingling fool seriously.

## The Jester's moves

Each move matches one of her finished flipbook animations, so the spell's length on screen is its cast time.

| Move | Animation | Length | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whisper Sigil | Orb, 24 frames at 6 fps | 4.0 s | Spends one secret from her pendant to mark a target with Doom |
| Raise the House | Skyward, 16 frames at 2 fps | 8.0 s | Bends fate for the party: a heal over time or a speed boost, stronger the worse things look |
| Curtain Call | Cast, 48 frames at 6 fps | 8.0 s | Her ultimate. Every enemy she has marked hits zero at once, then the sparkle ring wards the party |
| Bells at Her Hip | Walk, 16 frames at 6 fps | (walking) | Enemies always hear her coming, so the party is rarely ambushed, and she always acts first when a fight starts |

## MP balancing

All four use MP. Raw power is balanced by cost and cast time, not by capping how strong a spell can be.

| Character | MP pool | Spell cost | Cast time | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ranger | Large | Low | Instant | Steady damage, utility |
| Herbal Witch | Large | Medium | Short (summons long) | Healer, summoner |
| Fox | Medium | Medium to high | Short | Burst damage, tricks |
| Jester | Small | Very high | Long | A few casts that decide a fight |

- **Cast time is the lever.** The Jester's long animations are windows where enemies can interrupt her, so the party has to protect her while she casts.
- **Moon-phase discount (optional):** each character's spells cost less on her own phase, borrowed from the moon-phase brewing in Witch Way. The Jester peaks on new-moon nights, and the Ranger is easiest at the full moon.
