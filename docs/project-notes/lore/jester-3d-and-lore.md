## What the HD model has now

**Body states.** She holds one until told otherwise:
- Stand, walk (a true walk since pass 18), run.
- Kneel, squat.
- Sit on a stool she conjures, sit cross-legged on the ground.
- Hover cross-legged over a wheel of light.

**Moves** (play once, over any state):
- Jump.
- Talk: 11 seconds of hand gestures, with her mouth moving.
- Wave, bow, twirl (two turns on one foot), laugh, dance (a jig).
- Two she does by herself when left standing: look around, settle.
- Added in pass 19: caper (side leaps and a turn in the air), prance (high-kneed skips and a flourish), juggle (three small new moons).

**Spells.** All ten work from any state, so she can cast while kneeling, seated or hovering.
- Six keyed from her flipbook, with its timing:
  - Curtain Call (8 s)
  - Sealed Fate (4.8 s)
  - Against All Odds (8 s)
  - Gilded Rebuke (2 s, with her bells as a real swinging chain)
  - Hush (4.8 s)
  - Gravity (1.7 s)
- Four built from the move sheet's descriptions:
  - Ill Omen (2 s)
  - Stop (4 s)
  - Royal Reversal (3 s; two fingers lift an edge of her veil)
  - The Last Toll (5 s; a single giant bell)

**Moves from the lore,** added in pass 19 beside the ten spells:
- Big spell (5.4 s): Sealed Fate with a throw, her rings, and a closing fist.
- Big spell: the ending (12.9 s): the same, running on into Curtain Call.
- Attack taunt (6 s): Gravity dropped out of a jig and a twirl, then a laugh.
- Heal taunt (8 s): Against All Odds, danced and laughed through.
- Take a hit: two reactions, one laughing and one dusting herself off. Each blow adds to the dust round her.

**Her moves in the game,** added in pass 21 beside all of the above:
- Gilded Rebuke as her plain attack (2 s): the flipbook's move, with the blow thrown from the bells to the foe as a thread of gold.
- Sealed Fate (5.4 s): pass 19's big spell, with the sealed foe ended as the rings shut and a streak running to every other foe.
- Curtain Call (12.9 s): pass 19's ending, with the splash, the other foes taken with the first, and a light standing up where each fainted friend lay.
- The two taunts are used as they were. After either, three small new moons circle her: the blows she will still catch.

**Effects:**
- Sparks, mist and motes; glows and sigil sprites; marks on the floor; shock rings; a shaft of light.
- Spell-specific: the ring that sweeps round her waist in Curtain Call, the Hush ring, and the gravity well (a black heart with a violet rim).
- Room effects when a spell lands: the picture jolts or sways, light flashes, and motes cross the whole stage, a different kind for each spell (confetti for Curtain Call, silver rain for The Last Toll).
- Added in pass 19, for the lore's moves: the not-HD look for her power (holes with the night sky inside, thin cold rings, star dust, the place going dark while she stays lit), and glow on a switch.
- Added in pass 20: her charge, shown as a dust aura that thickens and draws together into the rings when she casts.
- Added in pass 21: Fate on a foe, shown as dust falling round it like sand. When the foe is sealed, the dust locks into a ring and a small eclipse hangs over it.

**Stage:**
- A small theatre with a worn stage floor and lanterns.
- Walk-around with a touch pad or keys.
- A practice dummy that rocks when hit.
- Quick buttons, and a Flipbook look that draws her as pixel art.
- Added in pass 19: the night square from the not-HD model, with its dummy. The theatre is still there on a switch.
- Added in pass 21, on its own page: two practice foes with a knight's and a wisp's numbers, a bar over each (Life, with Fate growing over it), and buttons for a blow, a heavy blow and a friend in peril.
