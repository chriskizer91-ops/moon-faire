## The world (proposed)

- **Setting:** Thareia, Chris's world, in the realm of Aethermoor: airships on sunstone lift, the Gloomfen, Bogmire, Misthollow and Ironhold. Chris decided in October that as many stories as possible will be set on Thareia, on its moon, or on one of the other planets in his lore.
- **The Moon Faire:** a festival airship touring Aethermoor. Its four performers are secretly its strongest casters, and each hides a weapon in her costume.
- **The moon rule:** all magic is moonlight. The less moon in the sky, the more of it a caster carries. Each witch draws on one phase.
- **Borrowed from Envoi into the Jester's story *A Fool's Mercy*** (`docs/stories/a-fools-mercy.md` in the repo):
  - Wisps are souls going hollow, and wraiths are what they become.
  - Defeat is release, not death: a pale moth rises out of the beaten foe.
- **Not named yet:** the four witches, the moon and the other planets.

## Io in Chris's other games

- ***Envoi on the Longest Night*:** the Witch is named Io, after the io moth and the moon Io.
  - She keeps Wickhollow's Moonwell, its youngest keeper. She is bookish, kind, short-sighted, and braver than she looks.
  - She calls Lunara, the Pale Mother: a giant moth who carries souls up the moonlight to the Moon.
  - She never burned the letters left for the dead. In that story she folds them into the paper dragon Envoi.
  - Envoi's Moon is **Auros**, alive and asleep. Its villain, **Noctara the Starless**, is the dark that was there before Auros woke.
- ***Follow Me Down Witch Way*:** "the witch" gathers herbs by moon phase and brews remedies for the living and the kindly dead of Wickhollow.
- **The first 3D model of her** is the file "Io, the Witch". Chris says it isn't very good.

## The Jester's 3D work so far

- **Not-HD game model,** six spells with effects (`claude/jester-game-model.md`):
  - Curtain Call: a magic circle raised into a chandelier of three, the stage dims, curtains of light, and her bow.
  - Sealed Fate: a seal drawn in her hand, thrown, and locked by three turning rings.
  - Against All Odds: a great circle, a column of light and climbing rings.
  - Gilded Rebuke: the bell chain whirled and lashed out.
  - Hush: a finger to her lips, a ring from her fingertip, and a shell closing on the target.
  - Gravity: a black ball dropped on the target under a falling column.
- **HD model** (`claude/jester-3d-pass15-16-moves-and-boots.md`, `claude/jester-3d-pass18-playable.md`):
  - Four more spells: Ill Omen, Stop, Royal Reversal and The Last Toll.
  - Other moves: hovering over a wheel of light, sitting on a stool she conjures, talking with gestures, bow, twirl, dance.
  - The HD flipbook also names Whisper Sigil and Raise the House.
- **The demo game *Night of No Moon: the square*,** built on Chris's November 11 rule that her power ends fights:
  - Doom releases a foe after three of its turns, counted by a chapel bell.
  - She was made the frailest of the four. Chris has since reversed that: her HP is the highest.
