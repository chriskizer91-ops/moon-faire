## Claude's view on the story (an opinion Chris asked for; nothing is decided)

The story could be about **an ending that never happened, and four ways of facing it.** Something on the moor never got its ending: the beacon died but its fire never went out, and the wisps are lights stuck halfway. The four witches' powers are four answers to that:
- The Ranger (the moment) sees what is really in front of them, now.
- Io (healing) wants to mend it.
- The Fox (chaos) wants to unpick it and sew it differently.
- The Jester (life and death, infinity) knows some things can only be given a good ending.

The beats outside combat are the four reading each place in their own way and disagreeing about what to do. The end needs all four in turn. It suits a world with a moon that barely moves and a fair that comes once in a lifetime, it sits on top of Opus's joined options A and C, and it needs no painting redone.
