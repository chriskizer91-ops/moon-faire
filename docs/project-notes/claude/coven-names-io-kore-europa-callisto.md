## The strongest links

- **Kore:** Persephone, queen of the dead who comes back each year. In Plutarch she rules the moon, where souls go after death.
- **Europa:** the first scene in Arachne's tapestry, a weaver's picture of reality so true it angered the gods. Carried across the sea where no one could follow.
- **Io:** "Io" was the name of the moon at Argos. The wanderer. The violet (Greek *ion*) is said to be named for her.
- **Callisto:** a huntress-princess in the band of Artemis. Her myth hangs on one stopped moment, and as the Great Bear she never sets.
- **Together:** all four names follow a moon in some way. Europa is Io's descendant. Ovid's *Metamorphoses* tells all four stories.
- **Open:** in each myth a god takes her. Whether the game uses that thread or keeps it offstage is Chris's choice.
