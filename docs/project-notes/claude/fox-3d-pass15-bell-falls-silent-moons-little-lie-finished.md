## The two spells

**When the Bell Falls Silent** (6.0 s). Painting J6, magic K1.

- She lays two fingers on her bell and holds it still.
- Her smile goes. Nine tails of foxfire open behind her like a peacock's fan.
- Blue flame lies along her shoulders and at her ears. The stage dims. One slow blink.
- She lets the bell go. It rings, and she smiles again.
- It is a change in her, not an attack. Nothing is hit.

**The Moon's Little Lie** (7.0 s, its light lands at 4.35 s). Paintings J7, J8, magic K2.

- A crescent no bigger than a coin spins on her raised fingertip. Her other hand is on her hip.
- She flicks it up. It climbs, grows and fills into a huge blue moon. The moon is your own painting K2.
- A needle sews it onto the sky, stitch by stitch round its rim.
- Arms wide, head back, her nine tails out. A shaft of its light falls on her target.
- Then the stitches come undone. The moon drops back into her open hand as a coin, and she closes her fingers on it. That is the lie.

## Where they differ from the paintings

- **J8:** four ghost doubles stand beside her. Here there are none. Each double costs drawing her again, and four would be too much for a phone.
- **J8:** the moon is straight behind her. Here it sits toward her target, so its light can fall there.
- **J6:** her tails are shorter and curl more. Here they are taller and straighter.
- **No teeth,** as everywhere.
