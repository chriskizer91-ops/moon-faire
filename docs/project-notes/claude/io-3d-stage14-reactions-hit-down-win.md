## What was built

A new row on the page, Her reactions, with three buttons. With these, every move planned for Io has a first build: seven spells and three reactions.

**Hit** (painting H17, left). 1.6 seconds. She staggers back from the blow with her body bent. Her right hand claps onto her hat to hold it on. The closed book is hugged tight to her chest. Her eyes are shut tight. She steps back, then recovers.

**Down** (H17, right). 5.6 seconds on the page. She sinks to the ground and sits on her hip with her legs folded to her left, leaning on her right hand, her head bowed and her eyes closed. Her hat tumbles off and lies on the ground beside her. The closed book lies under her left hand. On the page she gets up again after about three seconds so the next button works; in the game the pose would be held.

**Win** (H18). 3.8 seconds. Her weight on one hip, the open book on her left hand. She snaps it shut with a small puff of dust and holds it up beside her, tips the brim of her hat with her right hand, and gives her big delighted smile.

## Two things that had to be made for the hat to come off

- **The falling hat.** Her hat is part of her head in the model. To lose it, the worn hat is hidden and a copy of it tumbles to the ground.
- **Hair on top of her head.** Under the hat she had none. She now has tresses combed down each side from a middle parting, shown only while the hat is off. This was on the list of things still open ("hair on top of her head"). It is a first, plain version.
