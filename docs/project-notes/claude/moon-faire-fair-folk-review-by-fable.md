## What it changed (18 of the 19)

- **Scale.** Before, there were only a few fixed heights and the fortune-teller and lamplighter stood at 1.81 m, over the Jester. Now the folk run from 1.50 m (old lamp-seller) to 1.73 m (fortune-teller), the men's hats top out near 1.8 m, and the Jester sits in the middle.
- **Build.** The pie seller is sturdier, the ballad singer slimmer and younger-looking, the ribbon dyer smaller and less broad, the farm lad lankier.
- **Hats.** The Opus straw hat was three heads wide and is narrowed; two brims were lifted off the brows.
- **Hair and beards.** Hair made matt and darker where it shone like plastic; the Fable ring beard flattened on the baker and hidden on the dyer; the Opus roaster's block beard put back to the painted one.
- **Skin.** The dyer's hands and neck greyed to match his face.

One fault it found was in the kit, not the people: flat-coloured hair came out pale lilac whatever colour it was given. That was fixed in the kit afterwards (not by the reviewer).

Most valuable first:

3. **Beards.** The ring beard on the Fable baker and dyer shapes, and the box beard on the Opus dyer.
4. **The three women on the lamp-seller model share one outline.** A stoop and shawl for the old lamp-seller; a slimmer skirt and no work apron for the singer.

6. **Opus shapes.** The straw hat brim rebuilt and made solid; a notch in the baker's apron hem.
7. **The gate keeper shares the farm lad's body.** Her own outline (a long coat or skirt).
