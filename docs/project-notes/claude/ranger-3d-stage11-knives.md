## What is new

**Her two knives** (painting G5)
- Two alike, each one piece of moonsteel, 20 cm long.
- A ring at the end with a crescent moon standing in it.
- A short handle bound in dark blue cord, between two collars.
- A leaf-shaped blade.

**Their sheath** (worn as in painting G6)
- Black leather: two pockets side by side, a band across their mouths with a blue stud, two loops over her belt.
- At the small of her back, just under her belts, handles up.
- The folded bow now rides 2 cm higher, to make room for the knife rings.

**The "Knives" move** (painting H8)
1. Both hands go behind her and take a knife each.
2. She hops her feet wide, chest to us. Right hand up by her head, left hand low in front.
3. She coils, then throws the first knife with her right hand as her body turns toward screen right.
4. The second knife is already coming up. She throws it with her left hand as her body turns back.
5. Each knife flies point first with a thin trail of light.
6. She stands up. Her feet are planted through all of it.

**A choice Claude made, waiting for Chris's word**
- After the throw, the knives come back to their sheath in a small glint of light, so the move can be played again.
- If he would rather they stayed gone, that is a one-line change.
