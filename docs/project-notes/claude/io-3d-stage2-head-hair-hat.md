## The Flipbook look

## Third pass: what was done

- **Bangs frame her face.** Each lock leaves her parting under the hat, runs out across the top of her forehead above her
  brows to her temple, and from there lies along the side of her face, outside her cheek. Nothing crosses her brows, eyes,
  glasses or cheeks any more. 24 locks.
- **No gap.** The hair over her shoulders used to start above her ear. It now starts at her temple, so the bangs run straight
  into it. The tresses nearest her face are slimmer and wave less.
- **The hat is another 14 mm lower.** "Another 30%" was read as 30% of the same 4.8 cm gap as before, so the hat is now 45% of
  that gap lower than first built (21 mm). Its bottom edge in front is 2.7 cm above her glasses. If he meant 30% of what was
  left, that is 12 mm, 2 mm less.
- Lower down the hat met a wider part of her forehead and cut through it, so the foot of the crown is a little squarer at its
  corners and sits 11 mm further forward.

## Second pass: what was done for those five

- **No painted hair.** The front face picture had none. The side picture had fine strands across her temple, cheek, ear and
  neck; they are painted out.
- **The fringe is real hair.** It was flat strips lying on her skin. It is now 26 small tresses (13 a side) that leave her
  hairline under the hat, stand half a centimetre to nearly two clear of her skin, and hang: short ones stop above her glasses,
  longer ones pass outside the rims and hang in front of her cheek to her jaw. Every strand now ends in a point, so the tips of
  all her hair are fine wisps, not square teeth.
- **The hat is 7 mm lower.** From its bottom edge in front (1.577 m) to the top of her glasses (1.529 m) was 4.8 cm; 15% is 7 mm.
- **She blinks and talks.** Her shut eyes, half-shut eyes, parted lips, "oo" and smile are cut from C9 and set into her face.
  She blinks by herself; Talking runs her mouths with small nods. No teeth show in any of them.
- **The point under her chin is gone.** It was the profile fit overshooting at the lowest point of the chin. The chin is round.

## Where the paintings disagree, and what was chosen

- The whole-figure paintings (A1, B3, C4) draw a shorter, rounder face with bigger eyes than the close-ups (C1, C3, C8): 8.4
  to 9.1 cm from eye to chin against 10.3 cm. The close-ups are her face. She reads a little older and longer-faced than the
  small figures. Chris has since called her face "almost perfect", so the close-up face stays.
- Glasses: C2 and C14 show thin 44 mm rings; A1, C4 and every round 3 painting show bigger, darker ones. Built between the
  two: 50 mm, dark bronze, thin.
- C5 shows the crown's tail ending just below the brim; C4 shows it hanging to her chin. C4 was followed.
- C11 (no hat) shows hair in front of her arms from the front and inside her shoulders from behind. The model follows C5:
  hair in front of each shoulder and down her back, with her shoulders showing between.
- A1's eye line was first read 11 pixels too high (the top of her glasses). Nothing was built from it.

## Rough, and known

- Hair is stiff. It does not move, and with her arms raised it does not part round them. Up close a tress reads as a ribbon.
- The top of her head under the hat is bare (it shows only with the hat taken off).
- Her brows do not move, and the emotions in C10 are not in yet.
- The crown has soft creases, not the deep folds of the paintings. The lace band has no see-through edge.
- From stage one: stiff skirt with solid lace, the Jester's boot made taller, plain skin on arms, legs and hands.

## Tried and dropped

- Hair as one painted shell: not tried for her. Long loose waves need their own outline, so she went straight to tresses.
- Placing all hair by height alone: it pushed the hair at the side of her head round onto her face. Side hair has its own rule.
- A wide strip of the strand picture on each tress: two-toned tresses. Narrow strips, so a tress is dark or light.
- The first crown: right along its middle line, 5 cm too tall and too thin once beside the painting.
