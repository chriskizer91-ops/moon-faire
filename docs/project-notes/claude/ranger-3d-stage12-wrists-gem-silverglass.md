## Her wrists in the knife throw

- **The cause:** her hand was turned to suit the knife's written angle, whatever her forearm was doing. At the release that bent her wrist about 70 degrees, and more after it.
- **The fix:** her hand is now held within what a wrist can bend from her forearm. 34 degrees while she holds the knife, 12 once it has gone. The knife turns with her hand.
- **How it reads now:** cocked back a little in the wind-up, snapped forward at the throw, and after the knife has gone her open hand points where it went.
- The bow and the sword do not need it. Their grips were already within a wrist's range.

## The gem (painting H9)

1. Two fingers of her right hand touch the gem on her headband.
2. It flares, and a ring of light opens from it.
3. Light runs down the front of both her arms to her knuckles.
4. Her eyes close and she smiles.
5. For one breath everything hangs still: sparks in the air round her, her sleeves, her hair, her bags and charms.
6. Then it all lets go at once, and her hand comes down. Her arms stay lit for a moment.

**Against the lore.** In the agreed combat (`lore/combat-balance.md`) the gem is a move: two shots of 200, each aimed separately. This animation is only the touch that powers her up. The two shots after it are not built. Chris has been told.

## Silverglass (paintings H6, I2, I3)

1. She hops her feet wide and draws her left hand back.
2. She pushes her palm out flat, toward screen right and a little toward us. Her right hand is raised open behind it.
3. A round window of silver-blue glass a metre across opens in front of her palm. It has a crescent moon at its centre and loose shards floating at its rim.
4. She holds it.
5. It breaks into shards that scatter, turn, slow and fade.

It faces a little toward the camera so it shows as an oval, as in her painting. Facing straight at screen right it would be a thin line.

**Not decided.** Silverglass is not in the agreed combat. What it does to a blow, or to her next attacks, is not shown. The older idea (she shoots through it and the shards ride with her next attacks) was Claude's and has no yes from Chris.
