# Moon Faire: the task board (what is left, and who is on it)

Started November 14, 2026, 4:40 pm, by the open-world session, at Chris's request. **Every session working on the Moon Faire game reads this first and keeps its own rows up to date**, so that no two sessions work on the same thing.

## How to use it

1. **Read the whole board before you start.** Pick a row whose status is **Open** and whose "Needs" are all Done.
2. **Claim it before you do any work.** Read this note again, change only your row's status to `In progress: <your session's name>, <date and time>`, and write the note back straight away. Then read it once more to check your claim is there. The whole note is replaced on every write, so a slow write can wipe someone else's change: read, change, write, with nothing in between.
3. **Give your session a short name** that says what it does ("Fights", "Fox model") and use the same name every time.
4. **When you finish,** change the status to `Done: <date>` and put where the work is (repo branch and folder, and your own project note) in the last column. If you stop without finishing, set it back to Open and say what is left.
5. **If you are stuck on something only Chris can decide,** set `Waiting on Chris` and say what the question is.
6. **Change only your own rows.** Add a row if you find work nobody listed. Do not delete rows.
7. Keep the detail in your own project note. This board holds one or two lines a row.

Standing rules that apply to every row (from Chris, in the notes named at the end): three.js r128 with plain scripts; the witches' own light rig; lore is not final until Chris says so, and names are stand-ins; no witches in any painting; Fable 5.2 at high effort for building and Opus 5.5 for lore questions; his API key is never saved to the repo, the notes or memory.

**Changed by Chris on November 15 (`claude/chris-nov15-herbs-testers-foes-big-bad.md`, `claude/herbs-and-rest-spots.md`):**
- A fight no longer starts with full HP and MP. It starts with what the party has; Io's herbs, made from plants picked on the moor, refill it between fights with one press ("top everybody up"); a rest spot refills everything for free.
- **The shape of the game (1:06 pm):** wraiths for levels 1 to 10, then a gate, the final great wraith fight, around level 10. Envoi's two bramble foes with wraiths mixed in for levels 11 to 20, then a gate, the great bramble fight, at level 20. Then the final boss.
- Walking foes in the open land are from one level below the party to two above.
- Before the last fight the game offers easy, medium, hard and crazy: its foes at level 20, 21, 22 or 22.8, against a party that stops at 20.
- **The final boss has two forms (1:58 pm):** the first loses Life without showing damage, and at zero it changes into a final form that is broken, larger and more vibrant (`claude/final-boss-the-fire-that-would-not-end.md`).
- **The final fight (3:23 pm and 4:18 pm):** helpers first (wraiths and brambles), then the body alone. When form one falls the whole party comes back to full HP and MP, by the Jester's magic (life and death are her thing), and the final form is stronger. A party that falls in the final form starts again from before form one. A rest spot stands just outside the place of the fight (`game/plan/finalboss/final-fight.json`).

## Where the game stands

- **Walking and exploring:** a 3D open world, built and playable (`claude/moon-faire-open-world-map.md`). The fair, the moor, seven places, eleven fair folk who talk, wild plants, unseen random encounters that show a card where the fight will be.
- **Fights:** stay on painted backdrops. The pilot has one working fight with the Jester alone (`claude/starter-world-step3-4-pilot-jester-in-the-paintings.md`). The combat numbers are approved (`lore/combat-balance.md`).
- **Story:** chosen, "The fire that would not end", with 45 things that can happen written as data (`game/world/world.json`). None of them play yet.
- **Characters:** the Jester, the Fox and the Ranger are finished as models; Io is nearly there; the captain is being rebuilt. Only the Jester's light versions are saved as files.
- **Everything else** (saving, menus, sound, the title) is not started.
