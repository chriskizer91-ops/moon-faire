# A Fool's Mercy

A two-minute story for the Jester, using Envoi's foes in Envoi's night square. Written October 18, 2026, when Chris asked for a new story without airships, using Envoi's foes and town art. The film is `scenes/jester-square/`.

## The story

The Moon Faire has played its last show in town. The crowd has gone home, and the Faire's stall in the square is shut. One tune is still drifting from somewhere.

The Jester walks into the empty square. You hear her before you see her: everyone hears her coming, and nobody takes a jingling fool seriously.

The chapel bell tolls once. Wisps come out of the graveyard and out of the woods across the bridge. Wisps are souls starting to go hollow. They want light, so each one clings to a lamp, and the flames gutter.

One of them lunges at her. Gilded Rebuke: the bells at her hip turn out to be a flail. The wisp is released, and a pale moth rises out of it. That is how it goes for every lost soul in this world: defeat is release, not death.

The other two wail, and every lamp shakes. She lifts a finger to her lips. Hush. Their mouths are sealed with her silence mark, and the square goes quiet.

A Shadow Wraith climbs out of the graveyard: what a wisp becomes when nobody helps it. It raises its scythe. She doesn't wait for the swing. Gravity: a black new-moon disc presses it down to one knee, and the cobbles crack in a ring.

Then the great wraith comes over the bridge. It eats a town's light. It swallows every lamp in the square and the fire in the smithy, and the flames burn on in its ribs.

She listens to her pendant, which holds the secrets she has been told. Sealed Fate: a Doom sigil with a 3 appears over the great wraith, the Shadow Wraith and one wisp. The chapel bell counts it down.

The great wraith breathes the stolen fire at her. She gives ground, then raises her arms. Against All Odds: violet and gold stars fall, a gold ring turns at her feet, and the fire breaks on her light.

Curtain Call. The square goes dark like a theatre, and the moon turns into her new-moon disc. Sheer curtains close behind her, and she takes her bow in front of them. On frame 32, the chapel bell, her own bells and every Doom mark ring at once. Every lost soul is released as a moth, and the stolen flames fly home to their lamps.

The moon comes back. The moths rise past the bell tower toward it, the Faire's tune plays again, and the Jester walks up toward the chapel in the lamplight.

## What came from Envoi

- **The foes:** wisps, the Shadow Wraith and the great wraith, cut from Envoi's model and action sheets (`reference/art/`). The wraith sheet is violet, so its colours were turned soul-green, as Envoi's lore answer 18 says.
- **The square:** the night square painting, its matched camera, the lamps and the cut-outs she walks behind (`src/stage/night-square.js`).
- **Two ideas:** a wisp is a soul starting to go hollow and a wraith is what it becomes; and defeat is release, not death, shown as a pale moth rising (lore answers 16, 17 and 21).
- **Left out:** Noctara, Halcyon and anything Envoi has retired (the Drowned Mother, Old Snuff, the mandrakes, Mother's Hollow).

## Choices for Chris

- **The town's name.** In Envoi the square is Wickhollow's. The film never names it.
- **What the Jester is to lost souls.** Here her fate magic gives them an ending: Doom is a countdown to going home. Is that what Moon Faire's Doom means, or should it stay a plain death sentence?
- **Who sees it.** Here the Faire's other three performers are offstage. They could arrive for the curtain call.
- **The pendant of secrets.** Here it whispers something to her before Sealed Fate. The lore bible leaves open whether the secrets become a resource.
