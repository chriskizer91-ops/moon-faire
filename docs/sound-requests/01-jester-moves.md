# The Jester's moves

She's the New Moon. Her sounds are small gold bells, chiffon, a hidden gold flail, and dark moon magic that pulls and seals rather than burns. Lengths match her clips, and "release" is the moment the magic leaves her.

## Gilded Rebuke
- **Length:** 2 seconds. The flail strikes at 1.0 seconds.
- **When it plays:** she whips out her hidden flail and strikes a wisp.
```
A small jingle of gold bells, then a fast whip of a light chain flail through the air, striking at the one second mark with a bright metallic clang and a short ringing tail, the bells rattling after it. Close, dry, fantasy, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-gilded-rebuke.mp3`

## Hush
- **Length:** 5 seconds. Release at 2.7 seconds.
- **When it plays:** she raises a finger to her lips and seals a wailing spirit's mouth.
```
A soft breathy whisper of "shhh", then a thin glassy chime rising from B to the B an octave up, then at 2.7 seconds a quick airy swish as a small spell flies away, ending in a muffled soft thump and complete silence. Close, delicate, magical, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-hush.mp3`

## Gravity
- **Length:** 2 seconds. Release at 1.1 seconds.
- **When it plays:** she throws a small black orb, and a dark disc presses a foe down to its knees.
```
A low humming pull that builds for one second, a short dark whoosh as an orb is thrown, then a heavy downward slam into stone with a deep sub-bass thud and cracking cobblestones. Close, weighty, magical, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-gravity.mp3`

## Sealed Fate
- **Length:** 5 seconds. Release at 3.0 seconds.
- **When it plays:** her pendant wakes, she draws a sigil, and three glowing marks fly out to settle over three foes' heads.
```
A faint whisper and a shimmering high chime on E, G and B as a pendant glows, a magic sigil being drawn with a soft rising glassy tone, then at three seconds three quick swishes one after another, each landing with a small low bell toll on B. Close, eerie, magical, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-sealed-fate.mp3`

## Against All Odds
- **Length:** 8 seconds. Release at 1.5 seconds.
- **When it plays:** she throws her arms wide, a gold ring opens at her feet, and sparkles fall around her like warm rain.
```
A rising whoosh, then at 1.5 seconds a warm wordless choir swelling into a G major chord and holding, with a gentle rain of tiny gold sparkling chimes falling all around for six seconds, fading out at the end. Hopeful, magical, no drums, no melody, no music beyond the held chord.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-against-all-odds.mp3`

## Curtain Call
- **Length:** 8 seconds. Release at 5.2 seconds.
- **When it plays:** her biggest move. A drum roll builds, sheer curtains sweep shut behind her, and on the release they fly open as every lost soul goes home.
```
A theatre snare drum roll building faster and louder for five seconds, with silk curtains sweeping shut on both sides, then at 5.2 seconds a huge release: a deep church bell on B-flat, a cymbal crash, a bright wordless choir on a B-flat major chord, and a cascade of small bells falling down the B-flat major scale, ringing out to the end. Grand, joyful, theatrical, no melody.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-curtain-call.mp3`

## Steps on the cobbles
- **Length:** 1 second each. Make six takes.
- **When it plays:** every footstep, on her walks.
```
A single footstep of a light woman's boot on old cobblestones, with three tiny gold bells on her hip jingling as she steps. Close, dry, quiet, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `jester-step-1.mp3` to `jester-step-6.mp3`

## Her hip bells
- **Length:** 1 second each. Make four takes.
- **When it plays:** when she turns, bows or stands idle, and on the entrance before she's seen.
```
A small cluster of tiny gold bells jingling once, delicate and bright, like a jester's costume bells. Close, dry, no music.
```
