# Sound requests

These are prompts for an AI sound-effects maker, such as ElevenLabs' sound effects. Paste a prompt, set the length it gives, and download the result. They replace the sounds the films build in code: the songs stay, and these recorded sounds go on top of them.

- [01 — the Jester's moves](01-jester-moves.md): her ten moves, her steps and her hip bells.
- [02 — the foes](02-foes.md): Envoi's wisps, the Shadow Wraith and the great wraith.
- [03 — the square](03-square.md): the chapel bell, the lamps and the night itself.

## How to make them

1. Make each sound at the length given. If the tool asks how closely to follow the prompt, set it high.
2. Make two or three takes of each and keep the best one. For the steps and bells, keep every take you like, because a few versions stop them sounding like a loop.
3. Name each file as its "Save as" line says, and send them to me in the chat. They go in `art/sfx/`, and I time them to her frames.

## Notes for every sound

- **No music in the effects.** The songs carry the music. Every prompt below ends with "no music", and a take with a tune in it can't be used.
- **The keys.** Moonlit Forest Path is in B-flat major and Herbal Decay is in E minor. Anything with a pitch (bells, chimes, a choir) is given a note to match its song. If a take comes out in the wrong key, I can shift it in code.
- **Dry is better.** Ask for a close, dry sound. The films add the square's echo themselves, so they fit the camera.
- **Where her timing matters.** Her spells have a release point, the frame where the magic leaves her. Each prompt says how many seconds in that falls, so the big moment lands on it.
