# The foes

Envoi's lost souls, as they appear in *A Fool's Mercy*. They aren't evil. They're lost, and the Jester sends each one home as a moth. They should sound sad and cold, not like monsters.

## Wisp: arriving
- **Length:** 3 seconds.
- **When it plays:** a wisp drifts in from the graveyard or the woods road.
```
A ghostly whisper of many soft voices, too quiet to make out words, with a thin cold glassy tone sliding upward, like breath on a wine glass. Airy, sad, distant, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wisp-arrive.mp3`

## Wisp: clinging to a lamp
- **Length:** 4 seconds.
- **When it plays:** a wisp wraps round a street lamp and the flame gutters.
```
A candle-sized flame in an iron lantern sputtering and guttering, almost going out and catching again, with a faint cold whisper around it. Close, quiet, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wisp-cling.mp3`

## Wisp: the wail
- **Length:** 4 seconds.
- **When it plays:** two wisps wail together until the Hush stops them.
```
Two ghostly voices wailing together, high and wavering, a mournful wordless cry that rises and trembles, then cuts off sharply at the end into silence. Eerie, sad, not screaming, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wisp-wail.mp3`

## Wisp: struck
- **Length:** 1 second.
- **When it plays:** the gold flail hits a wisp.
```
A short ghostly yelp, a breathy cry knocked out of a spirit, with a brief cold shimmer. Close, quick, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wisp-hurt.mp3`

## Going home as a moth
- **Length:** 3 seconds. Make three takes.
- **When it plays:** a released soul turns into a pale moth and flutters up toward the moon.
```
Soft fluttering moth wings, light and quick, rising and fading away, with a gentle rising chime on B-flat. Peaceful, airy, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `moth-1.mp3` to `moth-3.mp3`

## Shadow Wraith: rising from the graveyard
- **Length:** 4 seconds.
- **When it plays:** the wraith climbs up out of the earth by the chapel.
```
Earth and stones shifting, a low rumble from under the ground, and a deep breathy hollow whisper rising with it, cold and slow. Ominous, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wraith-rise.mp3`

## Shadow Wraith: forced to kneel
- **Length:** 2 seconds.
- **When it plays:** Gravity presses it down to its knees.
```
A deep hollow groan of a spirit being pressed down, sinking in pitch, ending in a heavy settle. Sad rather than angry, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `wraith-kneel.mp3`

## Great wraith: coming down the road
- **Length:** 5 seconds.
- **When it plays:** the great wraith arrives from the woods road.
```
Heavy chains dragging on cobblestones, distant rolling thunder, and a huge slow breath like wind through a cave. Enormous, ominous, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `great-wraith-arrive.mp3`

## Great wraith: swallowing the lamplight
- **Length:** 2 seconds. Make one take for each of the six lamps, or one take used six times.
- **When it plays:** each street lamp's flame is pulled out and flies into its ribs.
```
A small flame being sucked out of a lantern with a quick airy inhale, a soft whoosh that ends in a muffled puff. Close, quick, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `lamp-swallowed.mp3`

## Great wraith: holding the fire
- **Length:** 10 seconds, made to loop.
- **When it plays:** while it holds every lamp's flame in its ribs.
```
A low steady roar of fire trapped inside something hollow, with soft crackles and a slow breathing swell. Seamless loop, dark, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `great-wraith-fire-loop.mp3`

## Great wraith: breathing fire
- **Length:** 5 seconds.
- **When it plays:** it breathes the stolen fire at her, and her ward holds it back.
```
A deep inhale, then a long roaring stream of fire breath that hits an invisible magic shield, crackling and spraying against it, then breaks apart in a bright burst of sparks at the end. Powerful, close, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `great-wraith-breath.mp3`
