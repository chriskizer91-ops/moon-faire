# The square

The night square itself: the chapel bell, the street lamps, the well and the quiet town.

## Night in the town
- **Length:** 30 seconds, made to loop.
- **When it plays:** under the opening and the walk home, before and after the trouble.
```
A quiet old town square at night: crickets, a light breeze, a faint creak of a hanging sign, very distant water dripping into a stone well. No voices, no traffic, seamless loop, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `town-night-loop.mp3`

## The cold wind the shades bring
- **Length:** 20 seconds, made to loop.
- **When it plays:** from the moment the wisps come until the Curtain Call.
```
A cold thin wind moving through narrow streets, with a hollow low moan under it and dry leaves skittering. Uneasy, seamless loop, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `shade-wind-loop.mp3`

## The owl
- **Length:** 2 seconds. Make two takes.
```
A single tawny owl hooting twice in the distance at night. No music.
```
- **Save as:** `owl-1.mp3`, `owl-2.mp3`

## The chapel bell: the first toll
- **Length:** 8 seconds.
- **When it plays:** once, as the trouble starts, while Moonlit Forest Path is ending.
```
A single strike of a large old church bell tuned to B-flat, ringing out long and slowly fading, heard from across a stone square. No music.
```
- **Save as:** `chapel-bell-bflat.mp3`

## The chapel bell: the Doom count
- **Length:** 8 seconds each. Two takes, one on G and one on F-sharp.
- **When it plays:** the bell counts down the sealed foes' Doom, from 3 to 2, then 2 to 1, over Herbal Decay.
```
A single strike of a large old church bell tuned to G, ringing out long, heavy and ominous, slowly fading. No music.
```
```
A single strike of a large old church bell tuned to F sharp, ringing out long, heavy and ominous, slowly fading. No music.
```
- **Save as:** `chapel-bell-g.mp3`, `chapel-bell-fsharp.mp3`

## A lamp lighting again
- **Length:** 2 seconds. Six takes, or one take pitched six ways in code.
- **When it plays:** after the Curtain Call, each lamp's flame flies home and lights with a little chime, climbing up the B-flat scale.
```
A small flame catching inside an iron lantern with a soft whump, and a single small clear chime ringing after it. Warm, close, no music.
```
- **Save as:** `lamp-relight.mp3`
